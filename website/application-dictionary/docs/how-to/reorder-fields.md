# Hide, rename or reorder fields

**To rearrange a whole form** use the [Field Layout Manager](../windows/fields.md):

1. Open *Field Layout Manager* and choose the entity.
2. Choose **Form Fields**. Drag a field card to another group, or up and down within a group.
3. Click the **eye** on a field to hide it from the form.
4. Choose **Save Layout**.
5. Choose **Grid Fields** to do the same for the list's columns.

![The Field Layout Manager](/img/fields-entity.jpg)

**To rename one field or change its help text** use [Window, Tab and Field](../windows/windows.md):

1. Open *Window, Tab and Field* and find the window.
2. Open the window, then its tab, then the field.
3. Choose **Edit**. Change **Name** (the label) or **Help** (the text behind the question-mark icon).
4. Choose **Save Changes**.

**To show a field only to look at, not edit,** turn on its **Read Only**. **To remove a column from the list** turn off **Displayed in Grid**.

The next request shows the change. If a field you changed does not move, check it is on the right tab and that [what the screens apply today](../what-is-applied.md) lists the setting you edited as applied.
