@ TaxCode
a taxType
s: Which tax this code belongs to, such as VAT, sales tax or withholding.
u: Chosen by tax staff when the code is set up; it decides which return the taxed transactions appear on and which rates are looked up.
c: Read together with the category, which says how that tax is treated on the line.
v VAT: Value-added tax charged at each stage of supply and reclaimed on purchases.
v GST: Goods and services tax, the value-added style tax used in countries such as Australia, India and Canada.
v SALES_TAX: Tax charged to the buyer at the point of sale and remitted by the seller.
v USE_TAX: Tax the buyer owes directly on purchases where no sales tax was charged by the supplier.
v WITHHOLDING: Tax deducted from a payment to a supplier or employee and paid over to the authority.
v EXCISE: Duty charged on specific goods such as fuel, alcohol or tobacco.
a description
s: Explains when this code should be chosen, including the kind of goods, services or customers it covers.
u: Read by whoever codes an order or invoice line to pick the right treatment.
c: For exempt and zero-rated codes it should cite the legal basis, so an auditor can see why no tax was charged.
@ TaxJurisdiction
a name
s: The name of the taxing authority's area, such as France, California or Cook County.
u: Entered by tax administrators and shown wherever a jurisdiction is picked, on tax reports and on registrations.
a level
v COUNTRY: A national tax authority covering a whole country.
v STATE: A state, province or region that levies its own tax.
v COUNTY: A county or district authority below the state.
v CITY: A city or municipality that adds its own local tax.
v SPECIAL_DISTRICT: A special taxing district, such as a transit or stadium district, with its own levy.
a filingFrequency
s: How often returns must normally be filed with this jurisdiction's authority.
u: Used to build the filing calendar and reminders for tax staff.
c: It may depend on the size of the business, so the registration states the frequency actually applicable to each company.
v MONTHLY: A return is due every month.
v QUARTERLY: A return is due every quarter.
v ANNUAL: A single return is due once a year.
@ TaxRate
a ratePercent
s: The tax as a percentage of the taxable amount, such as 20 for twenty percent.
u: Entered by tax staff from the authority's published rate when the rate type is a percentage, and applied to the taxable amount of each line.
a amountPerUnit
s: The tax charged per unit of quantity, such as a duty per litre or per kilogram.
u: Entered when the rate type is per unit and multiplied by the quantity on the document line.
a validFrom
s: The first day on which this rate may be applied to transactions.
u: Set from the law or the authority's announcement; it must not be later than the valid-to date, and a transaction uses the rate whose period holds its tax date.
a validTo
s: The last day on which this rate applies, left empty while it remains in force.
u: Set when a new rate replaces it; rates for one code and jurisdiction must not overlap, so the next rate starts the following day.
@ TaxRegistration
a taxType
s: The kind of tax this registration number was issued for, such as VAT or excise.
u: Chosen when the registration is recorded; it decides which returns the registration drives.
c: A company can hold several registrations in one country for different taxes.
v VAT: Registration for value-added tax.
v GST: Registration for goods and services tax.
v SALES_TAX: Registration to collect and remit sales and use tax.
v PAYROLL: Registration for payroll or employment taxes withheld from staff.
v CORPORATE_INCOME: Registration for tax on the company's profits.
v EXCISE: Registration to handle goods subject to excise duty.
a validTo
s: The last day on which the registration is valid.
u: Set when the company deregisters or the authority withdraws the number; left empty while the registration is in force.
a filingFrequency
s: How often returns are due under this registration, as set by the tax authority.
u: Drives the company's filing calendar and the reminders sent to tax staff.
v MONTHLY: Returns under this registration are due every month.
v QUARTERLY: Returns under this registration are due every quarter.
v ANNUAL: Returns under this registration are due once a year.
@ TaxTransaction
a taxTransactionNumber
s: The reference number of this tax record, used to find it on returns and audits.
u: Assigned automatically when the tax is calculated and never reused.
a documentType
s: The kind of source document the tax was computed on, such as a sales invoice or supplier bill.
u: Set by tax determination and read with the document reference to trace the tax back to its source.
a taxableAmount
s: The amount of the document line on which the tax was calculated, before the tax itself.
u: Copied from the document line in the document's currency and multiplied by the rate to give the tax.
a ratePercent
s: The percentage rate that was applied to the taxable amount.
u: Copied from the rate valid on the tax date and kept even if the rate later changes, so the original calculation can be explained.
a taxAmount
s: The tax computed on this line, after rounding.
u: Rounded under the jurisdiction's rules and reported in the return box of its tax code.
r taxCode
s: The tax code that was applied to the document line this record taxes.
u: Taken from the line when tax is determined.
n: Each tax record carries exactly one code, so a line taxed under two codes gives two records.
w: Its return box decides where the amount is reported.
@ Team
a purpose
s: A short description of what the team does and what work it should receive.
u: Free text entered by the administrator and read by people deciding where to route work.
a emailAddress
s: The shared mailbox that reaches the whole team.
u: Notifications addressed to the team are sent here instead of to each individual member.
a status
s: Whether the team is still operating and can be given work.
u: Set to inactive when the team is disbanded; inactive teams receive no new assignments.
v ACTIVE: The team is working and can be assigned work.
v INACTIVE: The team has been disbanded and receives no new work.
@ Timesheet
a periodStart
s: The first day of the period that the timesheet covers.
u: Usually a Monday; it must not be later than the period end.
a periodEnd
s: The last day of the period that the timesheet covers.
u: Usually a Sunday; one person's timesheets for the same engagement must not overlap.
a totalHours
s: All hours the person reported in the period, billable or not.
u: Summed from the daily entries and never negative; the billable hours cannot exceed it.
a submittedAt
s: The date and time the person submitted the timesheet for approval.
u: Recorded automatically on submission; late submissions are reported to managers.
a approvedAt
s: The date and time the approver accepted the timesheet.
u: Recorded automatically on approval; billing and cost allocation wait for it.
a rejectionReason
s: The approver's explanation of why the timesheet was sent back.
u: Written on rejection and read by the person to correct the entries before resubmitting; empty unless the timesheet was rejected.
r approvedBy
s: The person who reviewed and approved the timesheet.
u: Recorded when approval is given, usually the person's manager or the engagement lead.
n: A timesheet has one approver, who must not be the person whose time it is.
w: Approval by this person releases the hours for billing and costing.
@ TrackingEvent
a eventCode
v PICKED_UP: The goods were collected from the shipper.
v DEPARTED: The shipment left a hub, port or terminal.
v ARRIVED: The shipment reached a hub, port or terminal.
v IN_TRANSIT: The shipment is moving between two points.
v CUSTOMS: The shipment was held for inspection or released by customs.
v OUT_FOR_DELIVERY: The shipment is on the final vehicle heading to the consignee.
v DELIVERED: The shipment was handed over to the consignee, ending its journey.
v EXCEPTION: Something went wrong, such as damage, delay or a failed delivery attempt, and needs attention.
a description
s: The carrier's own wording of what happened, such as "Departed Novara hub".
u: Shown on the shipment timeline exactly as received, beside the standard milestone.
a source
s: Which channel supplied the event: a carrier feed, a vehicle device or a person.
u: Set when the event is recorded; manual events are checked before they change a delivery promise.
@ UsageRecord
a usageNumber
s: The reference number of this usage record, used when billing questions arise.
u: Assigned when the record is received and unique across all records.
a source
s: The metering system that reported the measurement, such as an API gateway.
u: Recorded on receipt and combined with the source reference to refuse duplicates when a feed is resent.
a quantity
s: How much of the metric was consumed in the measured period.
u: Expressed in the metric's own unit and rated against the subscription's plan to produce a charge.
@ Variance
a measure
s: What is being compared, such as travel expenses or revenue.
u: Set by the calculation, usually an account or account group, so the owner knows which line departs from plan.
a comparisonType
s: Which two figures are being set against each other.
u: Chosen by the calculation and decides which figure acts as the plan.
v ACTUAL_VS_BUDGET: Posted actual results are compared with the approved budget.
v FORECAST_VS_BUDGET: The latest forecast is compared with the approved budget to show expected overruns.
v ACTUAL_VS_FORECAST: Posted actual results are compared with the current forecast.
v FORECAST_VS_PRIOR: This forecast is compared with the previous forecast to show how expectations moved.
a status
s: How far the variance has progressed through explanation and review.
u: Moved on by the owner and by controlling; only reviewed variances are reported as explained.
v CALCULATED: Computed and waiting for the owner to explain it.
v EXPLAINED: The owner has written an explanation for the difference.
v REVIEWED: Controlling has examined and accepted the explanation.
v ACCEPTED: The difference is under the threshold, so no explanation is needed.
@ WarehouseZone
a status
s: Whether the zone is currently in use in the warehouse.
u: Changed during refits or closures; inactive zones receive no putaway.
v ACTIVE: The zone is open and can receive and release stock.
v INACTIVE: The zone is closed or being refitted and takes no new stock.
@ Wave
a waveType
s: The kind of work the wave releases to the floor.
u: Chosen when the wave is planned and decides which tasks it groups; most waves are picking waves.
a releasedAt
s: The date and time the wave was released to workers on the floor.
u: Recorded automatically at release and empty while the wave is still planned.
a completedAt
s: The date and time the last task in the wave was finished.
u: Recorded when the wave completes and compared with the planned completion to measure lateness.
a taskCount
s: How many tasks, picks and putaways together, the wave contains.
u: Calculated by the system and shown on the wave monitor so supervisors can size labour.
@ Yard
a yardType
s: The kind of facility the yard is, such as a terminal, depot or inland dry port.
u: Chosen when the yard is set up and decides which operations it supports.
c: Depots store empty containers and do not load vessels.
v TERMINAL: A port or rail terminal where containers are loaded and discharged.
v DEPOT: A depot that stores and repairs empty containers.
v INLAND: An inland dry port or container freight station away from the coast.
a capacityTeu
s: How many twenty-foot equivalent containers the yard can hold in total.
u: Equals the sum of its blocks' capacity and is used for utilisation and planning reports.
a operatingHours
s: The hours during which the yard gate is open, such as 24/7 or Monday to Friday 06:00 to 22:00.
u: Shown to hauliers booking gate slots.
@ YardBlock
a blockType
v GENERAL: Ordinary dry containers with no special handling needs.
v REEFER: Refrigerated containers that need an electrical connection.
v HAZARDOUS: Containers holding dangerous goods under segregation rules.
v EMPTY: Storage for empty containers awaiting reuse or repair.
v EXPORT: Containers waiting to be loaded onto a vessel.
v IMPORT: Containers discharged from a vessel and waiting for collection.
