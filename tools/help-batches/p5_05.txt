@ QualityCharacteristic
a dataType
s: The kind of value that observations of this characteristic take, which fixes how measurements are entered and checked.
u: Chosen when the characteristic is defined; quality measurements, forms and analytics follow it, and it should not change once measurements exist.
v DECIMAL: A numeric observation that may contain fractional values.
v INTEGER: A whole-number observation such as a count of defects in a sample.
v STRING: A textual or categorical observation represented as text.
v BOOLEAN: A true or false observation such as seal intact.
v ENUM: An observation selected from a governed set of allowed values.
a status
s: Whether this reusable characteristic definition is still being prepared, approved for use or retired.
u: Moved by quality governance; only ACTIVE characteristics can be assigned to new quality plans, and RETIRED is final but stays readable.
@ QualityInspection
a qualityInspectionId
s: The permanent, system-generated identity of this inspection or test record.
u: Generated on creation and never changed; measurements, samples, nonconformances and audit trails refer to the inspection through it.
a inspectionNumber
s: The business-facing reference quoted for this inspection in quality records and with suppliers.
u: Assigned when the inspection is raised and unique; used in supplier disputes, release decisions and quality reporting.
a status
s: Where the inspection stands from being raised through sampling to a final verdict.
u: Moved by inspectors; it controls release and disposition workflows, and passed, failed and cancelled are final.
v OPEN: Raised and not yet started; waiting for an inspector.
v IN_PROGRESS: Samples are being taken and measured.
v PASSED: The goods met every requirement. A final state.
v FAILED: The goods did not meet the requirements. A final state.
v CONDITIONAL: Passed only with conditions, awaiting a decision on release.
v CANCELLED: The inspection was not carried out. A final state.
a disposition
s: The decision on what to do with the inspected goods.
u: Set after a result is recorded; it drives inventory availability, supplier returns, repair, scrap and release.
v RELEASE: Release the goods for use or sale.
v ACCEPT: Accept the goods into stock.
v REJECT: Refuse the goods because they failed to meet requirements.
v QUARANTINE: Hold the goods apart until a decision is made.
v RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER: Send the goods back to the supplier.
v REWORK: Correct the goods so they meet the requirement.
v REPAIR: Repair the goods to a usable condition.
v SCRAP: Destroy or write off the goods.
v CONDITIONAL_RELEASE: Release the goods for use only under stated conditions that must be met.
a notes
s: The inspector's observations and supporting context that the recorded results do not capture.
u: Free text entered during or after inspection; read in quality reviews, supplier disputes and audits.
@ QualityMeasurement
a qualityMeasurementId
s: The permanent, system-generated identity of this single quality observation.
u: Generated on creation and never changed; traceability, audits, analytics and integrations refer to the measurement through it.
a measurementNumber
s: The human-facing reference that identifies this one measurement within inspection records.
u: Assigned when the measurement is captured and unique; used by reviewers, auditors and integrations to cite it.
a measuredValue
s: The numeric value actually observed during the inspection, such as a weight or temperature.
u: Entered for numeric characteristics together with the unit; compared with the limits to give the result, and exclusive with measured text.
a measuredText
s: The categorical or textual observation made, such as a colour grade or appearance remark.
u: Entered for characteristics that are not numeric; used in evaluation where a number does not apply, and exclusive with measured value.
a unitOfMeasure
s: The unit, such as kilograms or degrees Celsius, in which the numeric measured value is expressed.
u: Chosen with a numeric value; used to compare against limits, convert between units and report consistently.
a result
s: The verdict on this measurement when compared with its governing acceptance criterion.
u: Set when the value is evaluated; inspections use it to complete, raise nonconformances and feed quality analytics.
v PASS: The measured value is within its limits.
v FAIL: The measured value is outside its limits.
v CONDITIONAL: Within tolerance only under a stated condition.
v NOT_EVALUATED: No verdict has been given yet because it was not compared with a criterion.
a measuredAt
s: The date and time at which the observation was actually taken.
u: Captured with the measurement; supports sampling traceability, compliance checks and audit of when results were obtained.
a method
s: The method or procedure used to obtain the observation, such as a named test standard or instrument routine.
u: Entered by the inspector; lets others reproduce the test and lets laboratory audits confirm the right method was used.
a notes
s: Supporting context for this observation, such as sample condition or instrument remarks.
u: Free text entered by the inspector; read during review and audit to explain unusual values.
