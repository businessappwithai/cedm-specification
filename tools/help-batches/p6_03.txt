@ Result
a resultCode
s: The code that identifies one derived result, such as R-2210.
u: Assigned when the result is created and unique; used to search, report and exchange the result with other systems.
a resultType
s: The kind of derived output this result is, such as an IC50, a purity percentage or a pass/fail call.
u: Chosen by the analyst; it decides how the value is read and which reports and analyses include the result.
a value
s: The derived or interpreted outcome computed from the underlying observations, held as text.
u: Entered or calculated by the analyst; read in reporting and decisions, and locked once the result is final.
a status
s: Whether the result is still provisional, approved as final, or withdrawn as invalid.
u: Starts as DRAFT; set by the reviewing scientist, and a final result can no longer be edited, only invalidated.
v DRAFT: The result is provisional and may still be changed or discarded.
v FINAL: The result has been reviewed and approved, and its value is locked as evidence.
v INVALIDATED: The result is kept for the record but is no longer considered valid.
@ RetailSaleLine
a quantity
s: The number of units of the product sold on this line.
u: Entered by the cashier or counted by barcode scans; must be greater than zero and reduces the product's stock when the sale completes.
a lineAmount
s: The amount charged for this line after quantity, price, discount and tax have been applied.
u: Calculated by the till rather than typed; the lines are added together to give the sale total.
r product
s: The product or service the shopper bought on this line.
u: Chosen from the catalogue or identified by a barcode scan at the till when the line is added.
n: Each line sells exactly one product, though a product can appear on many lines across many sales.
@ RetailSale
a status
s: How far the sale has progressed from being rung up to being paid or reversed.
u: Starts as OPEN at the till and moves to COMPLETED on payment; a later refund or a void closes it.
v OPEN: Being rung up at the till; payment has not yet been taken.
v COMPLETED: Paid in full and the receipt issued to the shopper.
v VOIDED: Abandoned before payment was taken. A final state.
v REFUNDED: The goods were returned and the shopper was paid back. A final state.
@ ReturnDisposition
a quantity
s: How many units of the returned goods this disposition decision covers.
u: Entered by the returns handler; partial quantities allow one return to be split across outcomes, and inventory is adjusted by this amount.
a status
s: The stage of the disposition decision, from proposal through approval to physical execution.
u: Starts as DRAFT and is moved by the authorising user and warehouse; inventory movements are only allowed once it is AUTHORIZED.
v DRAFT: Being prepared; the outcome is not yet decided or approved.
v AUTHORIZED: Approved and ready for the warehouse to carry out.
v EXECUTED: Carried out and the stock adjusted to match. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was carried out. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Could not be carried out as authorised and needs a manual decision.
