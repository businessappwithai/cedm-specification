@ InventoryLocation
a status
v ACTIVE: Open for receiving, storing and picking.
v BLOCKED: Temporarily barred from movements, for example for a count or a quality hold; it can be unblocked.
v INACTIVE: Not in use; no stock should be placed there, though it can be reactivated.
r handlingUnits
n: Any number of handling units, such as pallets or cartons, that are currently in the location.

@ InventoryReservation
r product
n: Exactly one product: a reservation commits stock of a single item.

@ InventoryTransfer
r sourceLocation
n: Exactly one source: a transfer takes stock from a single location, and a multi-source move is several transfers.
r targetLocation
n: Exactly one target: a transfer delivers stock to a single location, and a multi-target move is several transfers.

@ Invoice
a invoiceNumber
s: The number printed on the invoice, by which customer and supplier refer to it.
a invoiceType
v SALES: An invoice the organisation issues to a customer for goods or services supplied.
v PURCHASE: An invoice the organisation receives from a supplier.
v CREDIT_NOTE: A document that reduces what is owed under an earlier invoice.
v DEBIT_NOTE: A document that increases what is owed under an earlier invoice.
a status
v DRAFT: Being prepared; not yet sent and not yet part of the receivables.
v ISSUED: Sent or recorded; the amount is now owed and due by its due date.
v PARTIALLY_PAID: Part of the amount has been settled by payments or credits.
v PAID: Fully settled. A final state.
v OVERDUE: Past its due date with an amount still outstanding.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it became effective. A final state.
v VOID: Cancelled after issue by a controlled process that keeps the original on record. A final state.

@ InvoiceLine
a matchingStatus
s: Whether the line has been checked against the order and receipt it bills for.
u: Set by the three-way matching process; lines in EXCEPTION block payment until they are resolved or waived.
v NOT_APPLICABLE: The line does not need matching, for example a sales invoice line.
v UNMATCHED: Not yet compared with the purchase order and goods receipt.
v MATCHED: Quantity and price agree with the order and receipt within tolerance.
v PARTIALLY_MATCHED: Some of the line's quantity or amount agrees; the rest does not.
v EXCEPTION: A difference outside tolerance needs a decision.
v WAIVED: The difference was accepted by an authorised approver.
