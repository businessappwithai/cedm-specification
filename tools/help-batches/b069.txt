@ Supplier
a supplierType
v INDIVIDUAL: A person supplying in their own name.
v BUSINESS: A commercial company.
v GOVERNMENT: A public body.
v INTERNAL: Another unit of the same group.
v OTHER: A supplier that fits no other type.
a qualificationStatus
v NOT_REVIEWED: Not yet assessed.
v PENDING: Assessment is under way.
v QUALIFIED: Approved to supply.
v SUSPENDED: Approval withdrawn for a time.
v DISQUALIFIED: Not approved to supply.
a status
v ACTIVE: Available for ordering.
v INACTIVE: Not currently used; can be reactivated.
v BLOCKED: Orders and payments are stopped.
v RETIRED: No longer used; kept for history. A final state.

@ SupplierCreditNote
a status
v DRAFT: Drafted by the buyer and not yet approved.
v APPROVED: Accepted and ready to post.
v POSTED: Recorded in the accounts; available to apply to invoices.
v PARTIALLY_APPLIED: Part of the credit has reduced invoices.
v FULLY_APPLIED: The whole credit has been used. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before posting. A final state.
v REVERSED: A posted credit undone by a reversing entry. A final state.
