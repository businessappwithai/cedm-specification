@ Scenario
a scenarioId
s: The identity of the scenario, constant for its life.
a status
v DRAFT: Being defined; not yet used in planning.
v ACTIVE: Available for budgets and forecasts.
v ARCHIVED: No longer used; kept for history. A final state.

@ Scrap
a scrapId
s: The identity of the scrap record, constant for its life.
a scrapNumber
s: The number of the scrap record, such as SCR-0412.
r workOrder
n: Each scrap record belongs to one work order, which carries the cost of the loss.
r product
n: A scrap record is for one product; scrap of several products is recorded separately.

@ SecurityIncident
s: A recorded event or condition that may compromise confidentiality, integrity, availability, safety or security.
b: An incident record is how the organisation notices, investigates and closes a security event. It keeps who found it, who handled it, how serious it was and when it was contained or resolved, so response can be reviewed and repeated problems seen.
u: Raised by whoever detects the event; investigated by security staff; read by management and auditors.
c: An incident belongs to an organisation, may name a reporter and investigator, and may relate to a recorded risk.
l: An incident is opened and investigated, may be contained and re-investigated, then resolved and closed. Closed is final.
x: Incident SI-2026-018 records phishing emails reported by finance staff, contained by blocking the sender and resolved after passwords were reset.
a securityIncidentId
s: The identity of the incident, constant for its life.
u: Generated on creation; actions and reports refer to it.
c: Referenced by investigation work.
a incidentNumber
s: The number of the incident, such as SI-2026-018.
u: Unique; quoted in reports and to regulators.
c: Read with the type and severity.
a incidentType
s: The kind of incident, such as phishing or data loss.
u: Entered when raised; reports group by it.
c: Decides which response procedure applies.
a severity
s: How serious the incident is.
u: Set at triage and revised as facts emerge.
c: Decides priority and who is told.
v LOW: Minor, with no real harm.
v MEDIUM: Limited harm that needs follow-up.
v HIGH: Serious harm or exposure.
v CRITICAL: Severe harm needing immediate action.
a status
s: Where the incident is in its handling.
u: Starts as OPEN; moved by the investigator.
c: Decides whether response work is still due.
v OPEN: Reported and not yet being worked.
v INVESTIGATING: Being investigated.
v CONTAINED: The spread has been stopped; cause still to be dealt with.
v RESOLVED: The cause is dealt with.
v CLOSED: Reviewed and finished. A final state.
a detectedAt
u: Entered from the report; response times are measured from it.
c: Compared with the resolution time.
a resolvedAt
u: Set when resolved; must not be before detection.
c: Gives the time to resolve.
r organization
s: The organisation affected by the incident.
u: Chosen when raised.
n: Exactly one organisation.
t: Decides who handles and is accountable.
r reporter
s: The person who reported the incident.
u: Chosen when raised.
n: At most one reporter; empty if automatic.
t: Who to ask for more information.
r investigator
s: The person responsible for investigating.
u: Assigned at triage.
n: At most one investigator.
t: Owns the response.
r relatedRisk
s: The risk in the register that the incident relates to.
u: Linked when an incident shows a risk has occurred.
n: At most one risk.
t: Allows the register to be updated.

@ SerialNumber
a status
v EXPECTED: Announced but not yet received.
v AVAILABLE: In stock and free to use.
v RESERVED: Set aside for an order or job.
v IN_TRANSIT: On its way between places.
v INSTALLED: Fitted at a customer or on equipment.
v CONSUMED: Used up. A final state.
v RETURNED: Sent back and awaiting a decision.
v QUARANTINED: Held apart pending a decision.
v SCRAPPED: Destroyed or written off. A final state.
v RETIRED: Taken out of service for good. A final state.
r product
n: Each serial number belongs to one product; the same number on another product is a different unit.

@ ServiceCase
a serviceCaseId
s: The identity of the case, constant for its life.
r requester
n: A case has one requester, the person to keep informed.
r tickets
n: Any number of tickets are worked under the case.
r serviceOrders
n: Any number of service orders may carry out the work.
