@ ReturnDisposition
a dispositionCode
v RESTOCK: Return the goods to saleable stock.
v QUARANTINE: Hold the goods apart until a decision is made.
v RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER: Send the goods back to the supplier.
v REWORK: Correct the goods so they meet the requirement.
v REPAIR: Repair the goods to a usable condition.
v SCRAP: Destroy or write off the goods.
v REJECT: Refuse the return; the goods go back to the sender.
v ACCEPT: Accept the goods as returned.
a status
v DRAFT: Being prepared; the outcome is not yet decided.
v AUTHORIZED: Approved and ready to be carried out.
v EXECUTED: The disposition has been carried out and stock adjusted. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was carried out. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Could not be carried out as authorised and needs a decision.

@ RiskAssessment
s: An evaluation of a risk's likelihood, impact, exposure, controls and residual risk at a defined point in time.
b: An assessment is the dated judgement on a risk. Keeping each one, rather than overwriting the risk, shows how exposure changed as controls were added and lets reviewers see what was known when a decision was made.
u: Performed by the risk owner or a reviewer; read by governance, audit and underwriting.
c: An assessment is of exactly one risk, and underwriting evaluations may draw on it.
l: An assessment is recorded once as at its date. A later reassessment is a new record; earlier ones stay as evidence.
x: In March the supplier concentration risk is assessed as possible and major; in September, after a second source, it is assessed as unlikely.

s: The identity of the assessment, constant for its life.
u: Generated on creation; reports and decisions refer to it.
c: Referenced when a decision cites the assessment.
a occurredAt
s: The date and time the assessment was made.
u: Set when recorded; the sequence of assessments is ordered by it.
c: Read with the risk to trace its exposure over time.
r risk
