@ ERP
a createdAt
s: The date and time the installation record was first created.
u: Set by the system when the record is created and never edited; it marks when the application context came into being.
a updatedAt
s: The date and time the installation record was last changed.
u: Set by the system on every change; it shows administrators when defaults such as currency or time zone were last altered.
@ EducationCourse
a name
s: The title of the course as students and staff know it, such as Introduction to Statistics.
u: Entered by the academic office; shown in catalogues, timetables and transcripts, so it should stay stable from term to term.
@ EducationProgram
a programType
s: The kind of qualification the program leads to, such as a degree, diploma or certificate.
u: Chosen when the program is created; it drives admission rules, credit requirements and how graduates are described.
v DEGREE: A university-level degree awarded on completing a full course of study.
v DIPLOMA: A diploma-level qualification, usually shorter or more applied than a degree.
v CERTIFICATE: A shorter certificate recognising completion of a focused body of study.
v COURSE: A stand-alone course that is treated as a program of its own.
v TRAINING: Vocational or workplace training that leads to a defined skill.
v OTHER: Any other kind of program that fits none of the above.
a status
s: Whether the program is being designed, open to students, closed for now, or withdrawn.
u: Set by the institution; only an ACTIVE program accepts new enrolments, and retired programs stay for graduates' records.
v DRAFT: Being designed; not yet open to students.
v ACTIVE: Open for enrolment and accepting new students.
v SUSPENDED: Temporarily closed to new enrolments, with current students unaffected.
v RETIRED: Withdrawn permanently but kept for graduates' records. A final state.
@ Emission
a quantity
s: The amount of gas or pollutant released, measured or calculated, in the unit the record states.
u: Entered from a meter reading or calculated from activity data; never negative, and it is totalled in sustainability reports.
r organization
u: Chosen when the emission is recorded; it decides whose carbon total and reporting boundary the emission counts towards.
r location
u: Set when the release can be tied to a site; site-level reports and regional limits read it, and it is left empty otherwise.
r product
u: Set when the release can be assigned to a product's production or use; product carbon footprints are summed through it.
