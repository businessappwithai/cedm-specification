@ Attendance
a attendanceId
s: The permanent system identity of one attendance record for a person on a date.
u: Generated on creation; payroll results, leave checks and corrections refer to the record through it.
a attendanceDate
s: The calendar date the presence, absence or work being recorded belongs to, regardless of when it was entered.
u: Entered by the person, supervisor or timekeeping device; payroll periods, class registers and reports group records by it.
a attendanceType
s: How the day is classified, such as present, absent, on leave, working remotely or a holiday.
u: Set at recording; leave must match approved leave evidence, and payroll and scheduling read it to treat the day correctly.
v PRESENT: The attendance subject participated or was present in the expected work or education context.
v ABSENT: The attendance subject was absent without another more specific governed category.
v LEAVE: Absence is attributable to governed leave.
v REMOTE: The attendance subject participated from an approved remote context.
v HOLIDAY: Date is treated as a governed holiday.
v OFF_DAY: Scheduled non-working day, such as a weekend or rostered rest day.
v OTHER: Another governed attendance classification.
a clockInAt
s: The moment the person started work or arrived, as captured by a clock, device or manual entry.
u: Optional; paired with the clock-out to derive worked hours, and compared with the schedule for lateness and overtime.
a clockOutAt
s: The moment the person finished work or left, as captured by a clock, device or manual entry.
u: Optional but never earlier than the clock-in; used with it to derive worked hours, overtime and early departure.
a workedHours
s: The number of hours counted as worked on the date, after breaks and rounding rules.
u: Calculated from the clock times or entered by a supervisor; non-negative, and read by payroll, utilisation and workforce reports.
a status
s: Whether the record is provisional, captured, or approved for payroll and other downstream use.
u: Moved by the person and their supervisor; payroll only relies on approved records and later corrections need corrective evidence.
@ BankTransaction
a description
s: The narrative text the bank supplied with the transaction, such as payer name, memo or payment details.
u: Stored as received; reconciliation staff read it to match the movement to a payment or investigate an exception.
@ Berth
a status
s: Whether the berth can currently take vessels, and with what limits, through to being permanently withdrawn.
u: Set by port planners as conditions change; vessel scheduling consults it before assigning a voyage, and safety restrictions are applied through it.
v ACTIVE: Open for ordinary vessel assignments.
v RESTRICTED: Usable only with limits, such as reduced draught or vessel length; new assignments need planner approval.
v OUT_OF_SERVICE: Temporarily unusable, for example for dredging or repair; no new assignments.
v RETIRED: Permanently removed from use; kept for history of past assignments. A final state.
@ Brand
a code
s: The short unique code identifying the brand across catalogue, sourcing and integration systems.
u: Assigned by master-data governance when the brand is created; used as the stable key when products and feeds name the brand.
a name
s: The brand's display name as customers and buyers know it, such as the manufacturer's trading name.
u: Shown on catalogue pages, documents and search results, and used to group sales and sourcing reports by brand.
a status
s: Whether the brand is in use, paused, or withdrawn from the catalogue.
u: Set by master-data governance; only active brands are offered for new products, and retired ones are kept for history.
v ACTIVE: In use and available for assignment to products.
v INACTIVE: Temporarily not offered, for example while a licence is renegotiated; it can be reactivated.
v RETIRED: No longer used; kept so historical sales and catalogue records remain accurate. A final state.
@ Budget
a status
s: How far the budget has progressed through preparation, approval, use and replacement.
u: Moved by the budget owner and approvers; only active budgets control spending, and later changes need a new revision.
v DRAFT: Being prepared by the budget owner; figures may change freely.
v SUBMITTED: Sent for approval; changes are held while it is reviewed.
v APPROVED: Accepted by the authorising body but not yet the live control.
v ACTIVE: The live budget against which spending is monitored.
v SUPERSEDED: Replaced by a later version; kept as the record of what was once approved. A final state.
v CLOSED: The period is over and the budget is closed for comparison with actuals. A final state.
@ BudgetLine
a budgetLineId
s: The permanent system identity of one allocation within a budget.
u: Generated on creation; variance reports and revisions refer to the line through it.
a amount
s: The money planned for this combination of account, cost centre and profit centre in the budget period.
u: Entered by the budget owner; never negative, and compared with actual postings to show overspend or underspend.
