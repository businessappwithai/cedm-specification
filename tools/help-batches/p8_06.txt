@ TestMethod
a code
s: The controlled business code identifying the test method, such as TM-HPLC-012.
u: Assigned when the method is authored; unique, and quoted on inspection plans, results and laboratory records to show which procedure was used.
a name
s: The readable name of the test method as quality and laboratory staff know it.
u: Entered by method owners; shown when planning inspections and recording results.
a instructions
s: The step-by-step procedure, equipment and acceptance approach that testers follow to produce valid evidence.
u: Written and reviewed under document control; testers follow it exactly, and changes are made through a new version, not silent edits.
a status
s: Shows whether the test method is being authored, available for use, paused, or withdrawn.
u: Moved by quality owners; only active methods may be selected for new inspections, and past results keep the method they used.
v DRAFT: The method is being written and cannot yet be used for governed work.
v ACTIVE: The method is approved and may be selected for new inspections and tests.
v SUSPENDED: Use is paused, for example pending a deviation or recalibration, and may resume.
v RETIRED: The method is withdrawn permanently and kept for history. A final state.

@ Ticket
a ticketNumber
s: The unique reference of the ticket, such as TK-20418, quoted by customers and support staff.
u: Assigned when the ticket is raised; used in queues, messages and SLA reports, and never reused.
a summary
s: A one-line statement of the problem or work the ticket asks for.
u: Entered when the ticket is raised; shown in queues and lists, so it should let an agent grasp the issue at a glance.
a priority
s: How urgently the ticket's work should be handled compared with other open tickets.
u: Set at creation and adjusted by the service desk; queues sort by it and SLA targets may depend on it.
v LOW: Minor impact that can wait behind other work.
v NORMAL: Standard urgency, handled in normal queue order.
v HIGH: Significant impact that needs prompt attention.
v URGENT: Severe impact needing immediate action ahead of everything else.
a status
s: Where the ticket stands from being raised through work to resolution and closure.
u: Moved by agents and the service desk; closed and cancelled tickets accept no further work.
v NEW: The ticket was raised and nobody has looked at it yet.
v ASSIGNED: The ticket has been given to a queue or person but work has not begun.
v IN_PROGRESS: An agent is actively working on the ticket.
v PENDING: Work is paused while waiting for the customer or a third party.
v RESOLVED: A fix or answer has been given and awaits confirmation.
v CLOSED: The resolution is confirmed and the ticket is finished. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The ticket was withdrawn without action. A final state.
