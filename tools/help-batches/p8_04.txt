@ Task
a code
s: The short business code that identifies the task in work queues, such as TSK-10482.
u: Assigned when the task is created; quoted in assignments, escalations and reports, and used to find the task without its full name.
a name
s: A short title stating what work the task asks someone or something to do.
u: Entered by the creator or the workflow that spawned it; shown in queues and notifications, so it should read as an action.
a description
s: Fuller instructions explaining what is to be done, why, and any details the performer needs.
u: Written by the creator; read by the assignee before starting, and updated if scope changes while the task is open.
a status
s: Where the task stands, from creation through assignment and execution to completion, cancellation or failure.
u: Moved by the assignee, workflow or system as work proceeds; completed, cancelled and failed tasks are closed to further work.
v CREATED: The task exists but is not yet ready to be picked up.
v READY: The task is released and waiting for someone to be assigned.
v ASSIGNED: A person or party has been given the task but has not started it.
v IN_PROGRESS: The assignee is actively working on the task.
v BLOCKED: Work is held up by a dependency, missing input or decision.
v COMPLETED: The work was done as required. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The task was withdrawn before completion. A final state.
v FAILED: The task ended without achieving its result and needs follow-up elsewhere. A final state.
a priority
s: How urgently the task should be worked relative to others in the same queue.
u: Set by the creator or workflow rules; assignees and queue views sort by it, and it may raise escalations when overdue.
v LOW: Can wait behind other work without business impact.
v NORMAL: Standard urgency, handled in the ordinary course of work.
v HIGH: Needs prompt attention ahead of normal work.
v CRITICAL: Needs immediate action because delay causes serious business impact.

@ TaxJurisdiction
a status
s: Shows whether this tax jurisdiction record is still being prepared, in force, or no longer in use.
u: Moved by tax administrators; only active jurisdictions are offered when determining tax, and completed or cancelled ones are kept for history.
@ TaxRate
a status
s: Shows whether the tax rate is being prepared, currently applicable, or withdrawn from use.
u: Moved by tax administrators; only active rates are picked up by tax calculation, and older rates stay for history.
@ TaxRegistration
a taxRegistrationId
s: The permanent system identity of one tax registration, unaffected by changes to the registration number.
u: Generated on creation and never changed; tax transactions and filings refer to the registration through it.
@ TaxRule
a name
s: The descriptive name of the tax rule, such as Standard VAT on domestic goods.
u: Entered by tax administrators; shown wherever a rule is chosen or explained, and should say what the rule covers.
a status
s: Shows whether the tax rule is being drafted, applied to transactions, paused, or withdrawn.
u: Moved by tax administrators; only active rules inside their validity dates are used to determine tax.
v DRAFT: The rule is being prepared and is not used for tax determination.
v ACTIVE: The rule is in force and applied to qualifying transactions within its validity dates.
v INACTIVE: The rule is switched off for now but can be reactivated.
v RETIRED: The rule has been withdrawn permanently and is kept for history. A final state.
