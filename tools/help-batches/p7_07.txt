@ SupplierCreditNote
a creditNoteNumber
s: The reference printed on the credit document, used by the supplier and by payables staff.
u: Entered from the supplier's document or assigned on recognition; unique; used to match the credit to statements and integrations.
a creditNoteDate
s: The effective date of the credit, which decides the accounting and tax period it falls in.
u: Entered when the credit is recorded; payable aging and tax reporting use it, and it can differ from the application date.
a status
s: The stage of the credit from preparation and approval through posting, application against invoices, or cancellation.
u: Starts as DRAFT; payables staff approve and post it, and the applied states follow the invoice applications made against it.
v DRAFT: The credit is being prepared and has no accounting effect.
v APPROVED: The credit is authorized but not yet posted to the ledger.
v POSTED: The credit is recognized in accounts payable and is available to apply.
v PARTIALLY_APPLIED: Part of the credit has been applied to invoices and some remains available.
v FULLY_APPLIED: The whole credit has been used against invoices.
v CANCELLED: The credit was withdrawn before posting and has no effect.
v REVERSED: The posted credit was undone by a reversing entry.
a currencyId
s: The currency in which every amount on the credit note is expressed.
u: Chosen when the credit is created, normally the original invoice currency; applying it to an invoice in another currency needs an exchange rate.
a subtotal
s: The credit value before tax, summed from the credit lines.
u: Calculated from the lines; tax is computed on it and it is reconciled with the lines before posting.
a taxAmount
s: The tax reversed or adjusted by this credit, summed from the lines.
u: Calculated from the credit lines; defaults to zero and is used in tax reporting and accounting.
a totalAmount
s: The total payable reduction the credit note represents, including tax.
u: Calculated as subtotal plus tax; it is the most credit that can be applied to invoices and is shown on supplier statements.
a amountUnapplied
s: The credit still available, being the total less what active applications have consumed.
u: Maintained as applications and reversals are recorded; payables staff use it to find credit to offset or request as a refund.
