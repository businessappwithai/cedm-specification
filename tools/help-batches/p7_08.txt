@ SupplierDebitNoteApplication
a appliedAt
s: The date and time the debit became effective against the target invoice.
u: Set when the application is activated and not changed; it decides the accounting period and appears on supplier statements.
a status
s: Whether this application of a supplier debit is being prepared, in force, reversed or cancelled.
u: Starts as DRAFT; payables staff activate it to reduce the invoice, which also changes the debit's remaining balance.
a reversalOfApplicationId
s: Points to the earlier application of supplier debit that this record undoes.
u: Set only on a correcting record; the original application is left unchanged so the audit trail stays complete.

@ SupplierDebitNoteLine
a supplierDebitNoteLineId
s: The stable identity of one line of a supplier debit, constant for its life.
u: Generated on creation; accounting, reconciliation and integrations refer to it even if line numbers change.
a lineNumber
s: The position of the line within its debit note.
u: Assigned in sequence when the line is added; unique within the note and used in review and reconciliation.
a unitPrice
s: The price per unit used to calculate a quantity-based debit.
u: Taken from the original commercial evidence such as the invoice line, not the current price list; blank for non-quantity debits.
a taxAmount
s: The tax associated with this debit line, zero where none applies.
u: Calculated from the gross amount using the original tax treatment; it feeds tax reporting and the net line amount.
a netAmount
s: The final amount of this debit line, being gross amount adjusted for tax.
u: Calculated; the debit note total is the sum of these amounts, and payables and supplier reconciliation read it.
r supplierDebitNote
u: Set when the line is created; the note supplies the supplier, currency and lifecycle that decide whether the line can be posted.
