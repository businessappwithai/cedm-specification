@ Workflow
s: A definition of how business activities and decisions are coordinated, run by people, by the system or by both.
b: A workflow is the agreed route a piece of work takes, such as an approval chain. Defining it once makes the process repeatable and lets each run be tracked, and a new version replaces it without disturbing runs already under way.
u: Designed by process owners; started by events or people; read by managers to track work.
c: A workflow may implement a business process and is made of tasks.
l: A workflow is drafted, active while it can be started, may be suspended, and is retired when replaced. Retired is final. A version is fixed once active.
x: The "Purchase approval" workflow routes orders over a threshold to the finance manager.
a workflowId
s: The identity of the workflow, constant for its life.
u: Generated on creation; runs and tasks refer to it.
c: Referenced by the tasks it creates.
a code
s: The code of the workflow, such as WF-PO-APPROVAL.
u: Unique; used when a workflow is started.
c: Read with the version.
a name
s: The name of the workflow.
u: Shown in lists and on tasks.
c: Read by process owners.
a workflowVersion
u: Increased when the workflow is changed; started runs keep their version.
c: Read with the code.
a executionType
s: Who carries out the steps.
u: Chosen when designed.
c: Decides whether tasks go to people or run by themselves.
v HUMAN: Steps are done by people.
v AUTOMATED: Steps run without a person.
v HYBRID: A mix of both.
a status
s: Whether the workflow can be started.
u: Starts as DRAFT; set by process owners.
c: Only active workflows start new runs.
v DRAFT: Being designed.
v ACTIVE: Available to start.
v SUSPENDED: Temporarily not started.
v RETIRED: Replaced or withdrawn. A final state.
a definitionFormat
s: The notation the definition is written in.
u: Chosen when designed.
c: Decides how the definition is read.
v CEDM: Written in the CEDM notation.
v BPMN: Written as a business process diagram.
v STATE_MACHINE: Written as states and transitions.
v OTHER: Written in another notation.
a definition
s: The definition itself: its steps, rules and routing.
u: Edited while a draft; fixed once active.
c: Must suit the declared format.
r businessProcess
s: The business process the workflow carries out.
u: Chosen when it implements a described process.
n: At most one process.
t: Links the executable design to the described process.
r tasks
s: The tasks that make up the workflow.
u: Read to see the steps.
n: Any number of tasks.
t: Tasks belong to the workflow.
