@ Opportunity
a opportunityNumber
s: The human-readable reference of the sales opportunity, such as OPP-2026-0142, used in conversation and reporting.
u: Allocated automatically on creation and kept unique; sales staff quote it in quotes, forecasts and pipeline reviews.
@ Organization
a organizationType
s: Classifies the organizational structure represented by the specialization, from whole enterprise down to department or branch.
u: Used for hierarchy, authorization, reporting, transaction scope, and organizational selection; chosen when the unit is created.
v ENTERPRISE: The top-level group or enterprise that owns every other organizational unit beneath it.
v COMPANY: A separate legal entity or operating company, usually with its own registrations, books and tax identifiers.
v BUSINESS_UNIT: A unit organized around a line of business or market, which may span several legal entities.
v DIVISION: A large internal division grouping departments under a common head or function.
v DEPARTMENT: A functional team within a company or division, such as finance or warehouse operations.
v BRANCH: A geographically separate office, store or site operating under a parent organization.
v SUBSIDIARY: A company controlled by a parent organization but trading as a separate legal entity.
v OTHER: A structure that fits none of the other types and is explained in its name or description.
a status
s: Lifecycle of the organizational specialization, deciding whether the unit may be selected in new transactions.
u: Controls whether the organization can normally be selected as an organizational scope; set by master-data staff.
v DRAFT: The organization is being set up and is not yet available for use in transactions.
v ACTIVE: The organization is in use and can be selected as an organizational scope in new records.
v INACTIVE: The organization is temporarily not selectable, for example while dormant, but its history is kept and it may return.
v RETIRED: The organization has been permanently closed or merged away and cannot be selected again. A final state.
a createdAt
s: The date and time at which the organization record was first created in the system.
u: Set once by the system on creation and never changed; used for audit trails and incremental synchronization.
a createdBy
s: The party, user or integration that created the organization record.
u: Set by the system from the authenticated actor on creation; used in audit to trace who introduced the record.
a updatedAt
s: The date and time of the latest change made to the organization record.
u: Refreshed by the system on every modification; used for synchronization with other systems and for audit review.
a updatedBy
s: The party, user or integration that made the latest change to the organization record.
u: Set by the system from the authenticated actor on each update; used in audit to identify who last changed it.
r addresses
s: The postal and contact addresses associated with the organization, such as legal, billing, shipping and service addresses.
u: Added by master-data staff as addresses are registered; selected on invoices, orders, deliveries and correspondence.
n: Any number of addresses, since an organization may have several sites and several purposes for each.
w: Supplies the addresses that billing, shipping, service and contact processes choose from for this organization.
@ Packing
a status
s: Shows how far the packing task has progressed, from planned through packed, or into cancellation or exception.
u: Updated by packers as work starts and finishes; shipping may proceed only once the task is packed.
@ PartyRelationship
a name
s: A readable description of the relationship, such as Acme Ltd is subsidiary of Acme Group.
u: Entered when the relationship is created; shown in party views and lists so users can understand the link at a glance.
@ PartyRole
a roleType
s: The kind of role the party plays, such as customer, supplier, employee or partner.
u: Chosen when the role is assigned; decides which specialised record (customer, supplier and so on) carries its detail.
v CUSTOMER: The party buys goods or services from the organization and is handled in sales and receivables.
v SUPPLIER: The party sells goods or services to the organization and is handled in procurement and payables.
v EMPLOYEE: The party works for the organization under an employment relationship.
v PARTNER: The party collaborates with the organization commercially, such as a reseller or alliance member.
v CARRIER: The party transports goods or people for the organization.
v AGENT: The party acts on behalf of the organization or of another party, usually for a commission.
v CONTRACTOR: The party provides labour or services under a contract rather than as an employee.
v OWNER: The party holds an ownership or beneficial interest in an asset or organization.
v INVESTOR: The party provides capital to the organization in return for a financial return or equity stake.
v OTHER: A role that fits none of the listed kinds and is explained in the role code or description.
a validTo
s: The last date on which the role still applies to the party.
u: Set when the role ends, such as a contract expiry; empty while open-ended, and later use of the role is refused.
a status
s: Whether the party currently holds the role and may be used in it.
u: Set by master-data staff; only active roles are offered in selections, and expired is reached when the validity ends.
