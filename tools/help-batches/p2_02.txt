@ Filing
a filingId
s: The permanent identity of the filing, unchanged by later corrections or supersession.
u: Generated when the submission is recorded; documents, receipts and follow-up filings refer to it as evidence.

@ Finding
a findingId
s: The permanent identity of the finding, kept even when its wording or status is later revised.
u: Generated when the finding is raised; remediation tasks, reviews and audit reports refer to it by this value.
a effectiveAt
s: The moment from which the finding stands as an issued observation about the control or risk.
u: Set at issue by the auditor or reviewer; ageing of open findings and remediation deadlines are measured from it.

@ FiscalPeriod
a fiscalPeriodId
s: The permanent identity of the accounting period, unchanged when its dates or status change.
u: Generated on creation; journal entries, close tasks and financial reports refer to the period by this value.
a code
s: The business code of the period, such as 2026-03 or FY2026-Q1, by which finance staff name it.
u: Assigned when the calendar is set up and shown on journals, close checklists and reports; unique within an organisation's calendar.
a startDate
s: The first accounting date that falls inside the period, from which postings are assigned to it.
u: Set when the calendar is defined; a journal entry is posted to the period whose dates contain its entry date.
a status
s: Controls whether journal entries may be posted to the period, from not yet open to finally closed.
u: Moved by finance through open, soft close, close and lock; journal validation reads it before accepting any posting.
v FUTURE: Defined but not yet open; no postings are accepted.
v OPEN: Open and accepting postings from ordinary finance users.
v SOFT_CLOSED: Normally closed but still open to postings by authorised finance staff, for adjustments.
v CLOSED: Closed; no further postings and figures are final for reporting. A final state.
v LOCKED: Frozen temporarily, for example during an audit or a month-end freeze; it can be reopened.

@ Flight
a status
s: Where the flight stands in its operation, from scheduling through boarding and departure to arrival or cancellation.
u: Updated by flight operations as the flight progresses; booking and passenger information read it to show delays and cancellations.
v SCHEDULED: Planned on the published schedule and open for booking.
v BOARDING: Boarding has started and passengers are being loaded.
v DEPARTED: The aircraft has left the origin and is in the air.
v ARRIVED: Landed at the destination. A final state.
v DELAYED: Departure is later than scheduled.
v CANCELLED: Will not operate. A final state.
