@ SupplierDebitNote
a supplierDebitNoteId
s: The identity of the debit note, constant for its life.
a status
v DRAFT: Drafted by the buyer and not yet approved.
v APPROVED: Accepted internally and ready to post.
v POSTED: Recorded in the accounts; available to offset what the buyer owes.
v PARTIALLY_APPLIED: Part of the debit has been offset against invoices.
v FULLY_APPLIED: The whole debit has been used. A final state.
v DISPUTED: The supplier disputes the debit.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before posting. A final state.
v REVERSED: A posted debit undone by a reversing entry. A final state.

@ SupplierDebitNoteApplication
a status
v DRAFT: Prepared and not yet applied.
v ACTIVE: Applied against the invoice, reducing what is payable.
v REVERSED: Undone by a reversing application. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was applied. A final state.

@ SupplierPerformanceAssessment
a supplierPerformanceAssessmentId
s: The identity of the assessment, constant for its life.
a status
s: Where the assessment is in review and publication.
v DRAFT: Scores and notes are still being compiled.
v IN_REVIEW: Under review by procurement or quality.
v APPROVED: Accepted and ready to publish.
v PUBLISHED: Shared with the supplier and used in decisions.
v SUPERSEDED: Replaced by a later assessment. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn. A final state.
a rating
v EXCELLENT: Consistently exceeds requirements.
v GOOD: Meets requirements with few issues.
v ACCEPTABLE: Meets the minimum required.
v NEEDS_IMPROVEMENT: Falls short in places; a plan is needed.
v UNSATISFACTORY: Fails requirements; action is required.

@ SupplierReturn
a returnNumber
s: The number of the return, such as RTS-0219.
a status
s: Where the return is, from authorisation to completion.
v DRAFT: Being prepared; not yet authorised.
v AUTHORIZED: Approved to be sent back.
v IN_TRANSIT: On its way to the supplier.
v RECEIVED_BY_SUPPLIER: The supplier has taken delivery.
v COMPLETED: Credit or replacement is settled. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was sent. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Problem with the return needing a decision.

@ SupplierReturnLine
a supplierReturnLineId
s: The identity of the return line, constant for its life.
a disposition
v RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER: The quantity is sent back for credit or replacement.
v REJECTED: The quantity is refused and stays with the supplier.
v EXCEPTION: The quantity is handled outside the normal return.
