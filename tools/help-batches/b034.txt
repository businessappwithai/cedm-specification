@ Observation
r results
n: Any number of results, since several results can be derived from the same primary observation.

@ OrganizationMembership
s: A relationship that connects a user or party to an organisation, with its membership status and role context.
b: People belong to organisations in different ways: as staff, partners, members or contractors. The membership says that a person belongs, in what capacity and whether it is current, which access and responsibilities are then based on.
u: Created when someone is invited or added; activated on acceptance; ended when they leave; read by access control and administration.
c: A membership links a User or Party to an Organization.
l: A membership is pending until accepted, active while in force, completed when it ends normally and cancelled if withdrawn before it began. State changes keep auditable history and do not duplicate effects.
x: A consultant added to a client organisation for the duration of a project.
a organizationMembershipId
s: The identity of the membership.
u: Generated on creation; role assignments and audit entries refer to it.
a status
s: Whether the person currently belongs to the organisation.
u: Moved by administrators or the invitation process; access based on membership applies only while ACTIVE.
v PENDING: Offered or requested, not yet effective.
v ACTIVE: In force; the person belongs to the organisation.
v COMPLETED: Ended normally. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it took effect. A final state.

@ Packaging
s: A packaging configuration that defines how a product or variant is contained, counted and handled for buying, storing, selling and shipping.
b: Goods are traded in packs, not just units: a carton of twelve, a pallet of forty cartons. The packaging record states these configurations so orders, stock and transport all count in the same units.
u: Defined by product management or logistics; chosen on purchase and sales documents; read by warehouse and transport.
c: Packaging belongs to a Product (and optionally a ProductVariant) and is measured in a UnitOfMeasure.
l: Packaging definitions are maintained over the product's life; changes apply to future documents while past documents keep what they used.
x: Carton of 12 bottles of 75 cl for the house red wine.
a packagingId
s: The identity of the packaging definition.
u: Generated on creation; documents and labels refer to it.
a code
s: The code of the packaging, such as CTN12.
u: Assigned by logistics; printed on labels and used in orders.
a quantityPerPackage
s: How many units of the product are in one package.
u: Entered by logistics; used to convert between packs and units. Greater than zero.
r product
s: The product that is packaged.
u: Set when the packaging is defined.
n: Exactly one product: a packaging configuration describes how a particular item is packed.
w: Lets orders and stock for the product be expressed in packs.
r productVariant
s: The variant, when the packaging is specific to one variant.
u: Set where the packaging differs by variant, such as size or colour.
n: At most one variant; packaging common to all variants leaves it empty.
w: Narrows the packaging to the correct variant.
r unitOfMeasure
s: The unit in which the package quantity is expressed.
u: Chosen when defined.
n: Exactly one unit, so the quantity per package is unambiguous.
w: Allows correct conversion between packs and base units.

@ Packing
s: A warehouse task that groups picked goods into handling units and prepares them for shipment, keeping line, lot, serial, quantity and package traceability.
b: Between picking and shipping, goods are put into boxes, cartons or pallets. Packing records which goods went into which package, so the customer's shipment is complete and any item can be traced to its package.
u: Created when picked goods are ready to be packed; completed when packages are closed; read by shipping and customer service.
c: Packing follows a Picking, fills a HandlingUnit and prepares a Shipment (and its lines).
l: Packing is planned, in progress while packages are filled, and packed when finished. It can be cancelled, or flagged as an exception when something does not match.
x: Eight cartons packed for order SO-20413 on the evening shift.
a packingId
s: The identity of the packing task.
u: Generated on creation; shipments and handling units refer to it.
a packedQuantity
s: The quantity of goods put into the package.
u: Entered or scanned as goods are packed; compared with the picked quantity.
a status
s: Where the packing task stands.
u: Updated by packers.
v PLANNED: Created, not yet started.
v IN_PROGRESS: Packers are filling packages.
v PACKED: Finished and ready to ship.
v CANCELLED: Called off. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Held because something is wrong, such as a shortage or damage.
r picking
s: The pick that supplied the goods.
u: Linked when packing follows a pick.
n: At most one picking.
w: Confirms that what is packed matches what was picked.
r handlingUnit
s: The package, carton or pallet that the goods go into.
u: Chosen when packing starts.
n: Exactly one handling unit: each packing task fills one package.
w: Gives the package contents and its identity.
r shipment
s: The shipment the package will be part of.
u: Linked when assigned to a delivery.
n: At most one shipment.
w: Adds the package to a delivery.
r shipmentLine
s: The shipment line that the packed goods satisfy.
u: Linked to tie packed goods to order lines.
n: At most one shipment line.
w: Shows which order line the goods fulfil.
