@ Employee
a status
s: Whether the person is currently working, away on leave, or has left the organisation.
u: Set by HR when employment changes; payroll and access rules read it, and the two leaver states close the record.
v ACTIVE: Employed and currently working for the organisation.
v ON_LEAVE: Employed but away on approved leave, so not currently working.
v TERMINATED: Employment ended by dismissal, resignation or end of contract. A final state.
v RETIRED: Left the organisation on retirement. A final state.
r person
u: Chosen when the employee is hired; personal details are read from that person and are not copied onto the employee.
r organization
s: The organisation that employs the person in this employee role.
u: Set by HR at hiring; payroll, reporting lines and headcount are read through it, and it stays empty for an unattached record.
n: At most one organisation, because an employee record belongs to a single employer at a time.
@ Employment
a employmentType
s: The contractual basis on which the person is employed, such as full time or on a fixed-term contract.
u: Chosen at hiring; it decides benefits, working time, notice periods and how the person is counted in workforce reports.
v FULL_TIME: A standard full-time contract with the organisation's normal working hours.
v PART_TIME: A contract for fewer hours than full time, usually with pro-rated pay and benefits.
v CONTRACT: A fixed-term or project contract that ends on a set date or at completion.
v TEMPORARY: Short-term or seasonal work covering a peak or an absence.
v INTERN: A student or trainee placement for learning rather than a permanent role.
v OTHER: Any other employment arrangement not covered by the listed kinds.
a status
s: Where the employment stands, from agreed but not started through in force to ended.
u: Moved by HR; pay, leave and attendance run only while it is ACTIVE, and a terminated one is kept for history.
r employee
u: Set when the employment is created; pay, leave and attendance are measured against this person's employment.
r employer
u: Chosen when the employment is created; it identifies the legal employer that carries the obligations of the contract.
r position
s: The position in the organisation structure that the person fills under this employment.
u: Assigned by HR at hiring and changed on a transfer; it places the person in a reporting line and cost centre.
n: At most one position, because someone fills a single position under one employment, and it may be unassigned.
