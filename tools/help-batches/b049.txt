@ Putaway
a status
v RELEASED: Released to the warehouse floor for a worker to carry out.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before the stock was moved. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Could not be completed as planned, for example the target was full; needs a decision.
r product
n: Each putaway moves one product; a mixed pallet is split into one putaway per product.
r sourceLocation
n: The stock comes from a single source location, such as the receiving dock.
r inventoryTransfer
u: Read to reconcile the putaway with the stock movement that physically relocated the goods.

@ QualityCharacteristic
a code
s: The short code of the characteristic, such as WEIGHT or PURITY.
a name
s: The name of the property being checked, as inspectors know it.

@ QualityInspection
a status
v OPEN: Raised and not yet started.
v IN_PROGRESS: Samples are being taken and measured.
v PASSED: The goods met every requirement. A final state.
v FAILED: The goods did not meet the requirements. A final state.
v CONDITIONAL: Passed only with conditions, awaiting a decision on release.
v CANCELLED: The inspection was not carried out. A final state.
a result
v PASS: Every measured characteristic was within its limits.
v FAIL: At least one characteristic was outside its limits.
v CONDITIONAL: Acceptable only if a stated condition is met.
a disposition
v RELEASE: Release the goods for use or sale.
v ACCEPT: Accept the goods into stock.
v REJECT: Refuse the goods.
v QUARANTINE: Hold the goods apart until a decision is made.
v RETURN_TO_SUPPLIER: Send the goods back to the supplier.
v REWORK: Correct the goods so they meet the requirement.
v REPAIR: Repair the goods to a usable condition.
v SCRAP: Destroy or write off the goods.

@ QualityMeasurement
a result
v PASS: The measured value is within its limits.
v FAIL: The measured value is outside its limits.
v CONDITIONAL: Within tolerance only under a stated condition.
