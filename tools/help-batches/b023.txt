@ IntegrationEndpoint
s: A logical endpoint through which messages or business events are exchanged with an internal or external system.
b: Systems talk to each other through addresses and agreed channels. An endpoint record names one such channel and how it is used, so that deliveries can be routed, retried and audited against a known destination.
u: Registered when a connection to another system is set up; referenced by delivery attempts; read by integration operators when diagnosing failures.
c: Delivery attempts target an endpoint, and messages are routed to it by subscription.
l: Delivery retries and corrections keep auditable evidence and never duplicate downstream business effects.
x: The partner's order-status webhook, to which the application posts "shipment.dispatched" events.
a integrationEndpointId
s: The identity of the endpoint, independent of its current address.
u: Generated on registration; subscriptions and delivery attempts refer to it, so the URL can change without breaking them.
a occurredAt
s: When the endpoint was registered or last took effect.
u: Set on registration or when its configuration changes; operators use it to tell which configuration applied to an old delivery.

@ Interest
s: An interest accrual or charge calculated for an eligible balance, on a rate basis, for a period.
b: Interest turns time and balance into money. Recording each calculation, with its inputs and period, lets finance explain what was charged or earned and recompute it if the basis is challenged.
u: Produced by interest runs on loans, deposits and receivables; posted to the ledger; read by finance and customers.
c: Interest refers to the balance and rate it was computed from and to the ledger entries that record it.
l: Finalised financial evidence is corrected by compensating or superseding records and never overwritten.
x: Interest of 41.67 accrued for March on a 10,000 deposit at a 5% annual rate.
a interestId
s: The identity of the interest record.
u: Generated by the interest run; postings and statements refer to it.
a effectiveAt
s: The date and time as of which the interest was calculated.
u: Set by the run, normally the end of the accrual period; it decides the period the interest belongs to.

@ InventoryAdjustment
s: An authorised correction of an evidenced stock discrepancy, whose quantity change is carried out by a stock movement.
b: Stock records and shelves drift apart. An adjustment is the controlled way of putting them back in line: it states why, by how much and who approved it, and the actual change is posted as a movement so the stock ledger stays a complete history.
u: Created after a count or investigation shows a discrepancy; approved; posted; read by warehouse management, finance and auditors.
c: An adjustment concerns one Product at one InventoryLocation, may arise from an InventoryCount, and results in an InventoryMovement.
x: A count finds 38 units where the system shows 40; an adjustment of minus 2 with reason "counting variance" is approved and posted.
a inventoryAdjustmentId
s: The identity of the adjustment.
u: Generated on creation; the resulting movement refers to it.
a adjustmentNumber
s: The document number of the adjustment.
u: Allocated from a number series; unique; used on approvals and audit reports.
a quantityDelta
s: The change to the recorded quantity: positive to add stock, negative to remove it.
u: Entered or derived from a count; posted as a movement when approved.
a reasonCode
s: The reason for the correction, such as counting variance, damage or theft.
u: Chosen from the organisation's list; reports analyse shrinkage by reason.
r product
s: The product whose recorded quantity is corrected.
u: Set when the adjustment is created.
n: Exactly one product: stock is corrected product by product.
w: Determines which stock balance is changed.
r inventoryLocation
s: The stock location where the quantity is corrected.
u: Set when the adjustment is created.
n: Exactly one location: the quantity belongs to a specific place.
w: Determines which location's balance is changed.
r inventoryCount
s: The count that revealed the discrepancy.
u: Linked where the adjustment follows from a physical count.
n: At most one count; some adjustments arise from damage reports or investigations.
w: Provides the evidence for the correction.
r inventoryMovement
s: The stock movement that carries out the correction.
u: Created when the adjustment is posted; read to trace the stock change to its authorisation.
n: Exactly one movement: each approved adjustment is posted as a single movement.
w: Keeps the stock ledger complete and the adjustment accountable.

@ InventoryBalance
a inventoryBalanceId
s: The identity of the balance record for one product at one location.
u: Generated when the first movement creates the balance; reservations refer to it.
r product
s: The product the balance is for.
u: Set when the balance is created.
n: Exactly one product: a balance is the stock of a single item.
w: Together with the location it identifies the balance.
r inventoryLocation
s: The stock location the balance is held in.
u: Set when the balance is created.
n: Exactly one location.
w: Together with the product it identifies the balance.
r inventoryMovements
u: Read to explain how the balance reached its current figure.
n: Any number of movements, all of which have contributed to the balance.

@ InventoryCount
a inventoryCountId
s: The identity of the count record.
u: Generated when the count is entered; adjustments refer to it.
a countNumber
s: The document number of the count.
u: Allocated from a number series; unique; printed on count sheets.
a countedQuantity
s: The quantity actually found on the shelf.
u: Entered by the counter; compared with the system quantity to find the variance.
a countedAt
s: When the count was made.
u: Entered with the count; the system quantity at that time is the comparison.
r product
s: The product that was counted.
u: Set when the count is entered.
n: Exactly one product: each count line observes one item.
w: Identifies which balance the count tests.
r inventoryLocation
s: The location where the count was made.
u: Set when the count is entered.
n: Exactly one location.
w: Identifies which balance the count tests.
r serialNumbers
s: The serial numbers found during the count.
u: Entered for serialised products.
n: Any number, one for each serialised unit found.
w: Verify that each tracked unit is where the records say.
