@ EnergyMeter
a meterType
s: The utility resource the meter measures, such as electricity, gas or water.
u: Chosen at registration; it fixes the unit of measure and which tariffs can be applied to the readings.
v ELECTRICITY: Electrical energy consumed or produced at the metering point.
v GAS: Natural gas consumed, usually read in volume or energy content.
v WATER: Water consumed, usually read in cubic metres.
v STEAM: Steam or district heat consumed.
v SOLAR: Electricity generated by solar installations and exported or used on site.
v OTHER: Any other metered resource not covered by the listed types.
@ EnergyTariff
a name
s: The descriptive name of the tariff, such as Business Peak and Off-Peak.
u: Chosen by the energy or billing team; shown on bills and in customer portals, so customers can recognise which rates apply.
@ Enrollment
a enrollmentNumber
s: The reference number of the enrolment, quoted by students and staff in correspondence.
u: Allocated by the registrar at registration and unique; fees, grades and certificates refer to it, and it never changes.
a status
s: Whether the registration is awaiting confirmation, in progress or ended, and how it ended.
u: Moved by the registrar; fees and attendance run while it is ACTIVE, and the three ended states are final.
