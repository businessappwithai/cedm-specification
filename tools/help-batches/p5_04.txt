@ Protocol
a protocolId
s: The permanent, system-generated identity of this research protocol, constant across its amendments.
u: Generated on creation and never changed; studies, experiments, assays and audit records refer to the protocol through it for reproducibility.
a title
s: The descriptive title of the protocol, naming the scientific procedure it defines.
u: Entered by the protocol author; shown in protocol lists, study plans and reports so scientists can pick the right method.
a status
s: Whether the protocol is cleared for new research execution, still being written, withdrawn or retired.
u: Moved by research governance; only APPROVED protocols can be applied to new studies, and RETIRED is final.
@ PurchaseOrderLine
a receivedQuantity
s: The total quantity of this line that has physically arrived from the supplier so far, accepted or not.
u: Increased by goods receipt lines; compared with the ordered quantity to show what is outstanding, and never negative.
a returnedQuantity
s: The total quantity of this line that has been sent back to the supplier after receipt.
u: Increased by supplier return lines; must not exceed what was accepted unless an exception is authorised, and does not reopen the original commitment.
a priceSource
s: Where the unit price on this line came from, such as a price list, a contract or a buyer's manual entry.
u: Recorded when the price is determined; supports audit, spend analysis and supplier negotiation by showing which agreement justified the price.
v PRICE_LIST: Taken from the supplier's published price list in force at the time.
v CONTRACT: Taken from a negotiated contract with the supplier.
v SUPPLIER_AGREEMENT: Taken from a standing supplier agreement covering repeat purchases.
v QUOTATION: Taken from a supplier quotation that the buyer accepted.
v MANUAL: Typed in by the buyer without a supporting agreement.
v OTHER: Determined in some other way not covered by the listed sources.
a priceDeterminedAt
s: The date and time at which the price on this line was fixed.
u: Recorded when the price is determined; lets later audits tell which effective-dated price applied even after price lists change.
@ PurchaseOrder
a orderNumber
s: The human-facing procurement reference quoted by the buyer and supplier for this order.
u: Assigned when the order is created and unique; used by buyers, suppliers, receiving, accounts payable and integrations to find it.
a status
s: Where the purchase commitment stands from draft through issue, receipt and closure.
u: Moved by buyers, approvers and receiving; controls authorisation, issuing, cancellation and closure, and cancelled and closed are final.
v DRAFT: Being prepared by the buyer; not yet binding on anyone.
v APPROVED: Authorised internally and ready to be sent to the supplier.
v SENT: Issued to the supplier, who is now expected to deliver.
v PARTIALLY_RECEIVED: Some of the ordered quantity has been received and accepted; the rest is outstanding.
v RECEIVED: Everything required has been received and accepted.
v CANCELLED: The remaining commitment has been withdrawn. A final state.
v CLOSED: Receiving and invoicing are complete. A final state.
a requestedDeliveryDate
s: The date by which the buyer asks the supplier to deliver the goods or complete the service.
u: Entered by the buyer and communicated to the supplier; receiving and planners use it to expect arrivals and chase late orders.
r organization
s: The buying organization that issues the order and is committed to pay for it.
u: Chosen when the order is created; decides which legal entity, budget, tax rules and approvals apply to the purchase.
n: At most one organization: the buyer is a single legal entity, and it may be unset on an early draft.
w: Identifies the buyer for authorisation, budgeting and reporting.
t: Ties the commitment to a legal entity for tax and budget.
r deliveryLocation
s: The place where the ordered goods are to be delivered or the service performed.
u: Chosen by the buyer; receiving, logistics planning and the supplier's dispatch use it to route the delivery.
n: At most one delivery location per order; a single order is delivered to one place and may be unset.
w: Tells receiving where to expect the goods.
t: Directs the supplier's dispatch to the receiving site.
@ Putaway
a putawayId
s: The permanent, system-generated identity of this warehouse task to store received or staged inventory.
u: Generated on creation and never changed; receiving, warehouse execution and the inventory movements it produces refer to it.
a quantity
s: The amount of stock the worker is directed to move into the target storage location.
u: Set when the task is planned and must be above zero; confirmation compares the quantity actually stored to this to show variances.
a status
s: Where this storage task stands on the warehouse floor, from proposed to confirmed.
u: Driven by warehouse orchestration and workers; completion needs movement evidence and completed and cancelled are final.
v PLANNED: Proposed by the system but not yet released to the floor.
v RELEASED: Released to the warehouse floor for a worker to carry out.
v IN_PROGRESS: Being carried out by a worker right now.
v COMPLETED: Confirmed by the worker and reconciled to inventory movements. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before the stock was moved. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Could not be completed as planned, for example the target was full; needs a decision.
