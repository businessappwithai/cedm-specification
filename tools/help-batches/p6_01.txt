@ QualityPlanCharacteristic
a lowerLimit
s: The smallest numeric measurement this plan accepts for the characteristic; anything below it fails.
u: Set by quality engineers when authoring the plan; compared with each measurement during inspection review and must not exceed the upper limit.
a upperLimit
s: The largest numeric measurement this plan accepts for the characteristic; anything above it fails.
u: Set by quality engineers when authoring the plan; compared with each measurement during inspection review and must not fall below the lower limit.
@ QuotationLine
a quantity
s: The number of units of the product or service offered to the customer on this line.
u: Entered by the salesperson, multiplied by the unit price to give the line value, and carried onto the order if the customer accepts.
@ RegulatorySubmission
a decisionAt
s: The date and time the regulatory authority announced its decision on the submission.
u: Recorded by regulatory affairs when the submission is approved or rejected; empty while it is pending, and used to track authority turnaround.
@ Renewal
a status
s: The stage the renewal has reached on the way to a new term.
u: Starts as DRAFT and is moved by account management as the customer is approached and a decision is reached; filters renewal pipeline reports.
v DRAFT: Being prepared by account management; nothing has been offered or committed to the customer.
v ACTIVE: Offered to or confirmed with the customer and now being carried out.
v COMPLETED: The new term is in place and the entitlement continues. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The customer chose not to continue, so no new term starts. A final state.
