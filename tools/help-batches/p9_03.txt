@ User
a username
s: The sign-in name the user types to authenticate, distinct from the permanent user identifier.
u: Unique across the application; chosen at registration or by an administrator and entered at every sign-in, so changing it affects how the person logs in.
@ Variance
a varianceId
s: The permanent identifier of the variance comparison, unchanged when its figures are recalculated.
u: Generated on creation; reports and commentary refer to it to point at one specific comparison of plan against actual.
a code
s: The reference code of the variance, such as VAR-2026-03-MKT, naming the period and area compared.
u: Assigned by finance or the planning system when the comparison is produced; used in reports, review packs and when quoting a variance to managers.
@ VehicleModel
a manufacturer
s: The company that builds vehicles of this model, such as a van or truck maker.
u: Entered once when the model is defined; used to filter and group the fleet and to look up manufacturer specifications.
a modelName
s: The commercial name the manufacturer gives the model, such as Transit 350.
u: Entered when the model is defined; shown in lists and pickers when vehicles are registered, so fleet staff can recognise it.
a modelCode
s: The unique code identifying the model in internal systems and manufacturer catalogues.
u: Unique; used in imports, parts lookups and integrations, so it should stay stable even if the model name is later reworded.
a modelYear
s: The year the manufacturer assigns to this version of the model, used to separate generations.
u: Optional; entered where different years of a model differ in specification, parts or tax treatment, and used to filter fleet reports.
a status
s: Whether the model is still being introduced, in current use or no longer offered.
u: Starts as PLANNED; moved by fleet or product managers as the model enters service and later leaves it.
v PLANNED: Expected to join the fleet or catalogue but not yet available for registering vehicles.
v ACTIVE: Current and available for registering new vehicles.
v DISCONTINUED: No longer offered or bought, though existing vehicles keep the model. A final state.
@ Vehicle
a registrationNumber
s: The registration or licence plate number that identifies the vehicle on public roads and in documents.
u: Unique; entered when the vehicle joins the fleet and used on delivery documents, inspections, fines and fuel records.
a vehicleType
s: The class of vehicle or equipment, such as truck, trailer or forklift.
u: Chosen on creation; dispatch uses it to match loads and tasks to suitable vehicles.
v TRUCK: A rigid powered road vehicle that carries goods in its own body.
v TRACTOR: A powered unit that pulls a trailer, carrying no load of its own.
v TRAILER: An unpowered load carrier towed by a tractor unit.
v FORKLIFT: A powered lifting vehicle used in yards and warehouses to move pallets.
v CRANE: Lifting equipment used to raise and move heavy loads such as containers.
v OTHER: Transport equipment that fits none of the listed classes.
a status
s: Whether the vehicle is available for work, being serviced, out of action or permanently withdrawn.
u: Starts as ACTIVE; moved by fleet managers, and dispatch only assigns work to vehicles that are active.
v ACTIVE: In service and available to be assigned to movements.
v MAINTENANCE: Temporarily withdrawn for servicing or repair and expected back.
v OUT_OF_SERVICE: Unavailable for work because of a fault, inspection failure or similar, with no firm return date.
v RETIRED: Permanently removed from the fleet. A final state.
@ Vessel
a vesselCode
s: The operational reference by which schedulers and port staff identify the vessel.
u: Unique; assigned when the vessel is registered and used in voyage planning, searches, reports and integrations.
a name
s: The registered name of the ship as shown on its papers and used in port communication.
u: Entered when the vessel is registered; shown on voyage schedules, berth plans and cargo documents.
a imoNumber
s: The seven-digit International Maritime Organization number that identifies a ship for life.
u: Entered where the vessel has one; used for compliance, port clearance and matching to external registries and tracking services.
a status
s: Whether the vessel is available for voyages, idle, out of action or permanently withdrawn.
u: Starts as ACTIVE; moved by operations staff, and scheduling only assigns voyages to vessels that are active.
v ACTIVE: In service and available for voyages.
v INACTIVE: Temporarily not used, for example laid up, but expected to return.
v OUT_OF_SERVICE: Unavailable because of damage, repair or a compliance problem.
v RETIRED: Permanently withdrawn from the fleet, for example sold or scrapped. A final state.
