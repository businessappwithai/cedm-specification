@ Timesheet
a timesheetId
s: The permanent identifier of the timesheet, unchanged however its hours or status are edited.
u: Generated by the system on creation; project costs and invoice lines point back to it to show which time record they came from.
@ TrackingEvent
a trackingEventId
s: The permanent identifier of one tracking observation, never reused or altered after the event is recorded.
u: Generated on creation; a correcting event cites it, and the shipment's history is assembled from these identifiers.
@ TradeDeclaration
a declarationType
s: The direction of the border crossing being declared, such as import, export, transit or re-export.
u: Chosen when the declaration is drafted; it decides which customs forms, duty rules and importer or exporter party apply.
v IMPORT: Goods entering the country or customs territory, on which duties and import taxes are assessed.
v EXPORT: Goods leaving the country or customs territory, declared by the exporter for control and export clearance.
v TRANSIT: Goods passing through a territory under customs control to a destination elsewhere, without being released for local use.
v RE_EXPORT: Previously imported goods sent out again, often to reclaim duties or after a temporary admission.
v OTHER: A crossing that fits none of the standard declaration kinds and is described in the supporting documents.
a status
s: Where the declaration stands in its dealings with the customs authority, from drafting through clearance.
u: Starts as DRAFT; moved as the declaration is submitted, reviewed and answered. Goods may not move until it is released.
v DRAFT: Being prepared by the declarant and not yet filed with customs.
v SUBMITTED: Filed with the customs authority and awaiting its acceptance or rejection.
v ACCEPTED: Customs has accepted the filing as valid and may still inspect it or release the goods.
v UNDER_REVIEW: Customs is examining the declaration or the goods and has not yet decided on release.
v RELEASED: Customs has cleared the goods to move. A final state.
v REJECTED: Customs has refused the declaration, which must be corrected and filed afresh. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The declarant withdrew the declaration before clearance. A final state.
@ TripSegment
a tripSegmentId
s: The permanent identifier of one leg of a trip, unchanged when its times or mode are revised.
u: Generated on creation; bookings and tickets for the leg refer to it so they can be matched to the right part of the journey.
a mode
s: The means of transport used to travel the leg, such as flight, train, road or sea.
u: Chosen when the leg is planned; it decides what must be booked, which supplier is used and what expenses to expect.
v AIR: Travel by aircraft, normally needing a flight booking and airport time.
v ROAD: Travel by car, van or truck on public roads, whether driven or hired.
v RAIL: Travel by train, booked as a rail ticket or pass.
v SEA: Travel by ferry or ship across water.
v BUS: Travel by bus or coach on a scheduled or chartered service.
v WALK: Travel on foot, usually a short transfer needing no booking.
v OTHER: Any means of transport not covered by the standard modes, described in the booking.
a status
s: How far the leg has progressed, from planning through booking and travel to completion.
u: Starts as PLANNED; moved as the leg is booked, started and finished, or called off before it ends.
v PLANNED: Intended as part of the route but nothing is booked yet.
v BOOKED: Tickets or reservations are confirmed for the leg.
v IN_PROGRESS: The traveller is making the journey on this leg now.
v COMPLETED: The leg has been travelled to its destination. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The leg was called off and will not be travelled. A final state.
