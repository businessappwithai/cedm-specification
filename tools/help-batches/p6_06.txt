@ SecurityIncident
a severity
s: How serious the incident is judged to be, based on the harm done or threatened.
u: Set at triage by the security team and revised as facts emerge; it drives escalation, response deadlines and notification duties.
v LOW: Minor, with no real harm or exposure.
v MEDIUM: Limited harm that needs follow-up but not urgent action.
v HIGH: Serious harm or exposure that needs prompt response.
v CRITICAL: Severe harm needing immediate action and senior escalation.
a status
s: How far the incident has progressed through handling, from report to review.
u: Starts as OPEN and is moved by the investigator as work proceeds; closure follows resolution and a review.
v OPEN: Reported and not yet being worked.
v INVESTIGATING: Under active investigation to establish what happened and how far it reached.
v CONTAINED: The spread has been stopped, but the cause is still to be dealt with.
v RESOLVED: The cause has been dealt with and normal service restored.
v CLOSED: Reviewed and finished. A final state.
@ SerialNumber
a serialCode
s: The code stamped on or issued for one physical unit, so it can be told apart from others of the same product.
u: Scanned at receiving, picking, shipment, service and return; unique per product and used for warranty and traceability.
a status
s: Where the individual unit stands in its life, from expected through stock and use to end of life.
u: Starts as EXPECTED or AVAILABLE and changes as the unit moves; it decides whether the unit can be picked, shipped or serviced.
v EXPECTED: Announced on an inbound order but not yet received.
v AVAILABLE: In stock and free to be reserved or shipped.
v RESERVED: Set aside for a particular order or job.
v IN_TRANSIT: On its way between locations or to a customer.
v INSTALLED: Fitted at a customer site or on equipment.
v CONSUMED: Used up in production or service. A final state.
v RETURNED: Sent back by a customer and awaiting a decision.
v QUARANTINED: Held apart pending a quality or safety decision.
v SCRAPPED: Destroyed or written off. A final state.
v RETIRED: Taken out of service for good. A final state.
@ ServiceCase
a caseNumber
s: The reference that customers and staff quote when talking about this case, such as CASE-004517.
u: Allocated automatically at intake and unique; used in searches, replies, escalations, reports and integrations.
a subject
s: A one-line description of the problem or request the case is about.
u: Written by the agent or taken from the customer's message; shown in queues, emails and search results.
a priority
s: How urgently the case needs attention, set by the business impact of the issue.
u: Chosen at triage by the agent; it orders queues and triggers escalation, separately from SLA clocks.
v LOW: Little impact; can be handled when capacity allows.
v NORMAL: Standard urgency, handled in the ordinary course.
v HIGH: Elevated business impact; handled ahead of normal work.
v URGENT: Serious impact; immediate attention is required.
a status
s: The stage the case has reached from intake through investigation to closure.
u: Starts as OPEN and is moved by the assigned agent; it drives SLA measurement, customer messages and reports.
v OPEN: The case has been accepted and is waiting to be worked.
v IN_PROGRESS: Active investigation or coordination is taking place.
v PENDING: Progress is waiting on the customer, a supplier or another dependency.
v RESOLVED: A resolution has been recorded and awaits confirmation and closure.
v CLOSED: The case is formally complete and kept as historical evidence.
v CANCELLED: The case was withdrawn through the governed workflow.
