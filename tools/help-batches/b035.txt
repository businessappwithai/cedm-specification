@ PartyRelationship
s: A time-bound relationship between two parties that describes how they stand to each other in business, legal, household, employment or another way.
b: Parties are connected to each other: a company to its subsidiary, a person to their employer, one household member to another. Recording the relationship, with its dates, lets the organisation see who is connected to whom and since when.
u: Created when a relationship is established; ended when it stops; read by sales, compliance and customer service.
c: A relationship runs from one Party to another.
l: Code and identity stay stable, and the relationship's history is kept when it ends or changes.
x: "Subsidiary of": Acme Ltd is a subsidiary of Acme Holdings from 1 January 2019.
a partyRelationshipId
s: The identity of the relationship.
u: Generated on creation; documents and reports refer to it.
a code
s: The code of the relationship type or instance, such as SUBSIDIARY_OF.
u: Chosen from the relationship list; used in queries and rules.
a name
s: A description of the relationship.
u: Shown in party views.
r fromParty
s: The party at the origin of the relationship.
u: Chosen when the relationship is created.
n: Exactly one origin party: a relationship always starts at a particular party.
w: Together with the other party it identifies the relationship.

@ PartyRole
a partyRoleId
s: The identity of the role, separate from the identity of the party.
u: Generated on creation; customer and supplier records refer to it.
a partyId
s: The party that holds the role.
u: Set when the role is assigned and not changed.
a roleType
s: The kind of role the party plays.
u: Chosen when the role is assigned; decides which specialised record (customer, supplier and so on) is expected.
v CUSTOMER: Buys from the organisation.
v SUPPLIER: Sells to the organisation.
v EMPLOYEE: Works for the organisation.
v PARTNER: A business partner or reseller.
v CARRIER: Transports goods for the organisation.
v AGENT: Acts on behalf of the organisation or its customers.
v CONTRACTOR: Provides services under a contract.
v OWNER: Owns property or a share in the organisation.
v INVESTOR: Provides capital.
v OTHER: Any other role.
a code
s: An optional code for the role.
u: Used by integrations that identify the role separately from the party.
a validFrom
s: The first date on which the role applies.
u: Set when the role is assigned.
a validTo
s: The last date on which the role applies.
u: Set when the role ends.
a status
s: Whether the role is currently held.
u: Set by master-data staff.
v ACTIVE: The party currently holds the role.
v INACTIVE: Dormant but may resume.
v EXPIRED: Ended; kept for history. A final state.

@ Payment
a paymentId
s: The identity of the payment, separate from the bank's reference.
u: Generated on creation; allocations, ledger entries and bank matches refer to it.
a paymentNumber
s: The number under which the payment is recorded and quoted.
u: Allocated from a number series; unique; shown on remittance advices and receipts.
a direction
s: Whether money is coming in or going out.
u: Set when the payment is created; decides whose account is debited and credited.
v RECEIPT: Money received from a payer.
v DISBURSEMENT: Money paid out to a payee.
a status
s: How far the payment has progressed from preparation to clearing.
u: Moved by treasury and the bank reconciliation process.
v DRAFT: Being prepared; no financial effect.
v APPROVED: Authorised but not yet recorded in the books.
v POSTED: Recorded in the ledger but not yet confirmed by the bank.
v CLEARED: Confirmed by bank evidence. A final state.
v VOID: Cancelled and of no effect. A final state.
v REVERSED: Cancelled after posting by a compensating entry. A final state.
a paymentMethod
s: How the money is moved.
u: Chosen when the payment is created; decides processing and fees.
v CASH: Notes and coins.
v BANK_TRANSFER: An electronic transfer between bank accounts.
v CARD: A payment card.
v CHEQUE: A paper cheque.
v DIRECT_DEBIT: A debit collected under a mandate.
v OTHER: Any other method.

@ PaymentAllocation
a paymentAllocationId
s: The identity of the allocation.
u: Generated on creation; reversals and ledger entries refer to it.
a status
s: Whether the allocation is in effect.
u: Moved by accounts receivable or payable.
v DRAFT: Prepared but not yet applied.
v ACTIVE: Applied; the invoice's outstanding amount is reduced.
v REVERSED: Undone by a compensating record. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before taking effect. A final state.
