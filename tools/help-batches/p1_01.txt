@ Country
u: Read by address forms, tax and trade rules, localization and reports; changed only by an administrator when the ISO registry changes.
a phoneCode
s: The international dialling prefix for the country, held without the plus sign, such as 44 or 1.
u: Used to validate and format telephone numbers entered against addresses and contacts, so the same number reads the same everywhere.
@ Coverage
a coverageId
s: The stable identity of one coverage component within a policy, unchanged for its whole life.
u: Generated when the coverage is created; claim lines and assessments point to it so the terms applied to a claim stay traceable.
a coverageCode
s: The business code naming the type of cover, such as collision, theft or liability.
u: Chosen from the insurer's product setup when a policy is written; claims are matched to the right cover by this code.
a description
s: A plain-language statement of what risk or benefit this coverage protects against.
u: Written by the underwriter when the policy is set up; read by servicing staff and shown to customers explaining their protection.
a status
s: Where the coverage stands: being written, in force, paused, ended by expiry or withdrawn.
u: Moved by policy administration; only an active coverage lets claims be assessed, while ended ones stay readable for past claims.
@ Credit
a status
s: Whether the credit is being prepared, available to use, used up, or withdrawn.
u: Moved by billing staff; only an ACTIVE credit can be applied to a charge or invoice.
v DRAFT: Being prepared by billing; it cannot yet be applied to any charge.
v ACTIVE: Issued and available to apply against what the customer owes.
v COMPLETED: Fully applied or expired, so nothing remains to use. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was used up, leaving no usable value. A final state.
@ CreditNoteApplication
a exchangeRateId
s: The exchange rate used to convert between the credit note's currency and the invoice's currency.
u: Filled only when the two currencies differ; it freezes the conversion so the application can be audited and reproduced later.
a appliedAt
s: The date and time the application took effect and began reducing the invoice.
u: Set when the application is activated; it decides which accounting period and customer statement show the reduction.
a status
s: Whether the application is being prepared, currently reducing the invoice, undone, or withdrawn.
u: Moved by receivables staff; only an ACTIVE application reduces the invoice, and the credit note and invoice keep their own statuses.
a reversalOfApplicationId
s: The earlier application that this record undoes, when it is a reversal.
u: Set when a wrong application is corrected; the original is never edited, so audit sees both the mistake and its correction.
