@ Picking
a pickingId
s: The identity of the pick task.
u: Generated on creation; packing and stock movements refer to it.
a quantity
s: The quantity to be picked.
u: Set from the reservation or order line; the pick is confirmed against it.
a status
s: Where the pick task stands.
u: Updated by warehouse staff and handheld devices.
v PLANNED: Created, not yet released to the floor.
v RELEASED: Released to pickers.
v IN_PROGRESS: A picker is collecting the goods.
v PICKED: All goods collected. A final state.
v SHORT: Less than the requested quantity was found; the pick can be retried after replenishment.
v CANCELLED: Called off. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Held because of a problem such as damage or a blocked location.
r sourceLocation
n: Exactly one source location: each pick collects from a single storage position.
r handlingUnit
n: At most one handling unit, the pallet or tote the goods are picked into.

@ Policy
s: A formally defined rule, principle or requirement that governs business behaviour or decision-making.
b: Policies state what the organisation has decided to do. Holding them as versioned records shows which version applied when, who owns it and which controls put it into practice.
u: Written by the policy owner; approved; made active; reviewed periodically; read by staff and auditors.
c: A policy is owned by an Organization, implemented by Controls and evidenced by a Document.
l: A policy is drafted, approved, then made active. It can be suspended and resumed, and is retired when superseded. Only an approved version may become active.
x: The Information Security Policy version 3.0, approved by the board and active from 1 January.
a policyId
s: The identity of the policy.
u: Generated on creation; controls and documents refer to it.
a code
s: The reference of the policy, such as POL-SEC-001.
u: Unique; used in audits and training.
a name
s: The title of the policy.
u: Shown in policy libraries.
a description
s: A summary of the policy's purpose and scope.
u: Written by the owner; read by staff.
a policyType
s: The area the policy covers, such as security, procurement or conduct.
u: Chosen at creation; groups policies for review.
a policyVersion
s: The revision of the policy text.
u: Incremented at each revision.
a status
s: Where the policy stands.
u: Moved by the owner and approvers.
v DRAFT: Being written.
v APPROVED: Approved but not yet in force.
v ACTIVE: In force.
v SUSPENDED: Temporarily not applied.
v RETIRED: Withdrawn. A final state.
a effectiveFrom
s: The first date the policy applies.
u: Set at approval.
a effectiveTo
s: The last date the policy applies.
u: Set when superseded; not before the start date.
r owner
s: The organisation accountable for the policy.
u: Set at creation.
n: At most one owner.
w: Gives accountability for review.
r controls
s: The controls that implement the policy.
u: Linked as controls are designed.
n: Any number of controls.
w: Show how the policy is put into practice.
r document
s: The document containing the policy text.
u: Attached on approval.
n: At most one document.
w: Provides the authoritative text.

@ Position
s: A defined role in an organisation, with its responsibilities, place in the reporting structure and staffing needs.
b: A position is a seat in the organisation chart. It exists whether or not someone sits in it, which lets headcount, budgets and succession be planned separately from the people.
u: Created by HR and management when a role is approved; filled by hiring; read by managers and recruiters.
c: A position belongs to an Organization, reports to a parent Position, may be based on a Job and is occupied by Employees.
l: A position is open until filled; it can become vacant again, be frozen while recruitment is held, closed, and finally retired.
x: Senior Accountant, Finance Department, reporting to the Financial Controller, filled in March.
a positionId
s: The identity of the position.
u: Generated on creation; employment records refer to it.
a code
s: The position number.
u: Unique; used in headcount plans.
a title
s: The title of the position.
u: Shown on charts and advertisements.
a status
s: Whether the position can be filled.
u: Set by HR.
v OPEN: Approved and vacant, or available to be filled.
v FILLED: Occupied by an employee.
v FROZEN: Held back temporarily and not to be filled.
v CLOSED: No longer needed but not yet retired.
v RETIRED: Removed from the structure. A final state.
a effectiveFrom
s: The first date the position exists.
u: Set when approved.
a effectiveTo
s: The last date the position exists.
u: Set when it is closed; not before the start date.
r organization
s: The organisation the position belongs to.
u: Set at creation.
n: Exactly one organisation: a position is a seat in one organisation.
w: Places it in the structure.
r parentPosition
s: The position this one reports to.
u: Set to build the reporting line.
n: At most one parent; the top position has none.
w: Defines the reporting chain.
r employees
s: The employees who hold the position.
u: Read to see who fills it.
n: Any number, since a position can be shared.
w: Link people to the seat.
r job
n: At most one job, the standard description the position is based on.

@ Practitioner
a practitionerId
s: The identity of the practitioner role.
u: Generated on registration; orders and encounters refer to it.
a practitionerCode
s: The unique code of the practitioner in the healthcare organisation.
u: Assigned at credentialing; used on orders and schedules.
a status
v DRAFT: Being credentialed; not yet cleared.
v ACTIVE: Cleared to practise.
v SUSPENDED: Barred pending review.
v INACTIVE: Not currently practising here.
v RETIRED: No longer a practitioner. A final state.
r person
n: Exactly one person: the professional role is held by a specific individual.
r appointments
n: Any number of appointments booked with the practitioner.
r encounters
n: Any number of encounters in which the practitioner took part.
