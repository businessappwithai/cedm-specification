@ SupplierCreditNoteApplication
a status
s: Whether this application of supplier credit is being prepared, in force, reversed or cancelled.
u: Starts as DRAFT; payables staff activate it to reduce the invoice, and a reversal is recorded as a new application.
a reversalOfApplicationId
s: Points to the earlier application of supplier credit that this record undoes.
u: Set only on a correcting record; the original is never edited, so the history of the credit stays intact for audit.

@ SupplierCreditNoteLine
a lineNumber
s: The position of this line within its supplier credit note.
u: Assigned in sequence when the line is added; unique within the note and used to refer to the line in review and posting.
a quantity
s: The quantity of the original goods or service that this credit line relates to.
u: Entered from the returned or adjusted quantity; it must reconcile with the supplier return line when the credit follows a return.
a grossAmount
s: The credited value before discount and tax, normally quantity times the original unit price.
u: Calculated from quantity and unit price; it feeds the taxable amount and is checked in payable reconciliation.
a discountAmount
s: The part of the original discount that is reversed or adjusted on this credited value.
u: Defaults to zero; copied from the original invoice treatment so the credit can be reproduced and reconciled.
a taxableAmount
s: The base amount on which tax is reversed or adjusted for this credit line.
u: Calculated as gross amount less discount; tax reporting and accounting read it to compute the tax component.
a taxAmount
s: The tax reversed or adjusted on this credit line, zero when no tax applies.
u: Calculated from the taxable amount using the original invoice tax treatment; it is added into the net line amount and tax reports.
r supplierCreditNote
s: The credit note document to which this line belongs.
u: Set when the line is created and not changed afterwards; the note supplies the supplier, currency and posting state.
n: Every line belongs to exactly one credit note and cannot exist on its own.
w: Whether a line may still be edited or posted depends on the lifecycle state of its parent note.
r supplierReturnLine
u: Chosen when the credit is caused by a return; it supplies the approved returned quantity that the credit may not exceed.
r invoiceLine
u: Chosen so the credit can copy the original price, discount and tax; payables staff use it to trace the credit back to the invoiced amount.
