@ AIPolicy
a status
v DRAFT: Being written and reviewed; not yet enforceable.
v ACTIVE: Adopted and enforced for the model it governs.
v COMPLETED: Superseded or run its course; no longer enforced for new decisions but kept as the record of past ones. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was adopted, so it never governed any model decision. A final state.
@ Appointment
a appointmentType
s: The kind of healthcare service the visit is booked for, such as a follow-up consultation, vaccination or imaging slot.
u: Chosen by the scheduler when booking; drives how long a slot is held, which resources it needs and how capacity is reported.
a scheduledStartAt
s: The date and time at which the patient is expected to be seen, in the clinic's planning calendar.
u: Entered when the appointment is booked and changed on rescheduling; reminders fire from it and lateness is measured against it.
a scheduledEndAt
s: The planned end of the slot, giving the time the practitioner and room are held.
u: Optional; when given it must fall after the start. Capacity planning uses it to detect overlapping bookings for the same resource.
a status
s: Where the booking stands, from being scheduled through attendance to closure as completed, cancelled or missed.
u: Moved by front-desk staff and clinicians as the patient confirms, arrives and is seen; reminders and capacity reports filter on it.
v SCHEDULED: Appointment is booked but not yet confirmed by the patient or provider.
v CONFIRMED: Patient or provider confirmation has been recorded.
v CHECKED_IN: Patient arrival or equivalent readiness has been recorded.
v COMPLETED: Scheduled service concluded and any required encounter evidence is reconciled. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The appointment was cancelled by the patient or provider before it took place. A final state.
v NO_SHOW: Patient did not attend according to policy. A final state.
@ Assay
a assayId
s: The permanent system identity of one assay execution, unchanged however the run is later described or corrected.
u: Generated on creation; observations, results and instrument records point back to the run through it.
a assayCode
s: The laboratory's unique reference number for this assay run, such as ASY-2026-0418.
u: Used to find the run, to match instrument output to it, and to cite it in reports and audits.
a assayType
s: The scientific kind of procedure performed, such as an ELISA, a potency test or a HPLC purity run.
u: Chosen by the analyst when planning; routes the run to the right instrument and bench and groups results for analytics.
a status
s: Where the run is, from planned through execution to a completed, failed or cancelled outcome.
u: Moved by the analyst or instrument integration as work starts and ends; results are only trusted from completed runs.
v PLANNED: Assay is prepared and scheduled but not yet started.
v RUNNING: Execution is underway and observations are being captured.
v COMPLETED: Execution concluded successfully and its results can be relied on. A final state.
v FAILED: Execution failed and its evidence is retained for investigation. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Execution was called off, either before starting or part way through. A final state.
@ Assessment
a assessmentType
s: The form the evaluation takes, such as a quiz, formal exam, coursework assignment, project, practical or oral.
u: Chosen by the educator; the gradebook uses it to weight and group results and learners see it when planning their work.
v QUIZ: Short-form evaluation, usually low weighted and taken in class.
v EXAM: Formal examination taken under controlled, timed conditions.
v ASSIGNMENT: Coursework submitted by the learner for marking.
v PROJECT: Project-based evaluation completed over an extended period.
v PRACTICAL: Practical or laboratory evaluation of applied skills.
v ORAL: Oral evaluation, such as a viva or spoken presentation.
v OTHER: Another governed assessment type not covered by the listed forms.
a maxScore
s: The highest raw score a learner can earn on this assessment, for example 100 for a percentage-marked exam.
u: Set by the educator when numeric scoring applies and must be positive; grades are expressed against it to compute percentages.
a dueAt
s: The deadline by which learners must submit or sit the assessment.
u: Set when the assessment is drafted or published; learner reminders use it and late-submission policy is judged against it.
a status
v DRAFT: Being prepared by the educator; not visible to learners and not gradeable.
v PUBLISHED: Active for eligible learners, who can see it and be graded on it.
v CLOSED: Assessment is complete for ordinary new grading. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Assessment was withdrawn and will not be graded. A final state.
