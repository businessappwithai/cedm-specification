@ TaxRate
s: A tax percentage or amount that applies under a tax code and jurisdiction for a period of time.
b: A tax rate is the figure applied to a taxable amount. Because rates change by date and place, each is recorded with its period so a transaction is taxed at the rate in force on its date, and past calculations can be explained.
u: Maintained by tax staff when authorities change rates; read by tax calculation.
c: A rate may belong to an organisation and applies to a tax code in a jurisdiction.
l: A rate is drafted, active while in force and completed when superseded. It may be cancelled before use. Completed and cancelled are final.
x: The standard rate for code STD in the United Kingdom is twenty percent from 4 January 2011.
a taxRateId
s: The identity of the tax rate, constant for its life.
b: Lets tax lines point at the rate that was applied.
u: Generated on creation; tax lines refer to it.
c: Referenced by every tax calculation that used it.
a status
s: Whether the rate can be applied.
b: Stops an out-of-date rate being used on new transactions.
u: Starts as DRAFT; set by tax staff.
c: Only active rates are picked up in calculation.
v DRAFT: Being defined; not yet applied.
v ACTIVE: In force and applied to transactions.
v COMPLETED: Replaced or expired. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before use. A final state.
r organization
s: The organisation the rate is set up for.
u: Chosen when the rate is company specific.
n: At most one organisation; empty means it applies to all.
w: Decides which company's returns carry the tax.

@ TaxRegistration
s: The registration of a party or organisation with a tax jurisdiction and its tax authority.
b: A registration is the tax identity the business holds in a place, such as a VAT number. Recording it shows where the business may charge and must report tax and which number to print on invoices.
u: Maintained by tax staff; read when invoicing and filing returns.
c: A registration may belong to an organisation and is held in a jurisdiction.
l: A registration is drafted, active while valid and completed when it ends. It may be cancelled before use. Completed and cancelled are final.
x: The company's VAT registration in Germany, active since March 2022.
a taxRegistrationId
s: The identity of the registration, constant for its life.
b: Lets documents and returns point at the registration.
u: Generated on creation.
c: Referenced by returns and invoices.
a status
s: Whether the registration is valid for use.
b: Prevents tax numbers that have lapsed from appearing on documents.
u: Starts as DRAFT; set by tax staff.
c: Only active registrations are printed on invoices.
v DRAFT: Applied for or being recorded; not yet valid.
v ACTIVE: Valid and in use.
v COMPLETED: Ended, for example after deregistration. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before use. A final state.
r organization
s: The organisation that holds the registration.
u: Chosen when recorded.
n: At most one organisation.
w: Decides which company files returns.

@ TaxRule
s: A reusable tax determination policy that sets the treatment, rate, jurisdiction and applicability for qualifying goods, services, parties and transactions.
b: Tax rules say what tax applies to what. Keeping them as dated policy lets the business change treatment on a given date without editing past transactions, and lets tax be explained from the rule that produced it.
u: Maintained by tax staff; read by pricing and invoicing when working out tax.
c: A rule may name a jurisdiction and the products, customers and organisations it applies to.
l: A rule is drafted, active while in force, may be set inactive and back, and is retired when replaced. Retired is final.
x: Rule "UK standard VAT" applies twenty percent to standard-rated goods sold to UK customers from 1 January 2011.
a taxRuleId
s: The identity of the rule, constant when it is renamed.
u: Generated on creation; tax lines refer to it.
c: Referenced by every tax line it produced.
a code
s: The code of the rule, such as UK-VAT-STD.
u: Unique; used in imports and reports.
c: Read with the name.
a name
s: The name of the rule.
u: Shown when a tax treatment is chosen.
c: Read by tax staff.
a rate
s: The rate applied, as a fraction of the taxable amount.
u: Entered by tax staff; applied to the taxable amount.
c: Read with the tax type.
a jurisdictionCode
s: The code of the jurisdiction the rule applies in.
u: Entered to narrow the rule to a place.
c: Matched to the transaction's place.
a taxType
s: The kind of tax the rule works out.
u: Chosen when the rule is created; returns are grouped by it.
c: Decides which return the amount goes to.
v SALES: A sales tax charged at the point of sale.
v VAT: Value added tax charged at each stage.
v GST: Goods and services tax.
v USE: A tax on use of goods bought without sales tax.
v WITHHOLDING: Tax held back from a payment.
v OTHER: A tax of another kind.
a validFrom
s: The date and time the rule takes effect.
u: Set when the rule is created.
c: Compared with the transaction date.
a validTo
s: The date and time the rule stops applying.
u: Set when the rule is superseded.
c: Compared with the transaction date.
a status
s: Whether the rule is in use.
u: Starts as DRAFT; set by tax staff.
v DRAFT: Being defined; not applied.
v ACTIVE: In force and applied.
v INACTIVE: Not applied for the time being.
v RETIRED: Replaced or withdrawn. A final state.
r jurisdiction
s: The place the rule applies in.
u: Chosen when the rule is place specific.
n: At most one location.
t: Limits the rule to transactions there.
r products
s: The products the rule applies to.
u: Chosen to narrow the rule.
n: Any number of products; none means all.
t: Decides which goods the rule taxes.
r customers
s: The customers the rule applies to.
u: Chosen when treatment depends on the customer.
n: Any number of customers.
t: Allows exemptions for particular customers.
r organizations
s: The selling organisations the rule applies to.
u: Chosen when only some companies use it.
n: Any number of organisations.
t: Limits the rule to certain companies.

@ TaxTransaction
s: The tax consequence of a taxable business transaction, kept apart from payment and ledger posting.
b: A tax transaction records how much tax arose on a sale or purchase and under what treatment. Keeping it on its own lets tax be reported and checked without changing the payment or accounting records.
u: Created automatically from taxable documents; read by tax returns and audit.
c: A tax transaction may belong to an organisation and follows a taxable document.
l: A tax transaction is drafted, becomes active when reportable and completed once reported. It may be cancelled before. Completed and cancelled are final.
x: The 20 tax charged on invoice INV-3041 is recorded as a tax transaction for the March VAT return.
a taxTransactionId
s: The identity of the tax transaction, constant for its life.
b: Lets returns and the source document point at the same tax record.
u: Generated on creation.
c: Referenced by returns.
a status
s: Where the tax transaction is in reporting.
b: Shows whether the tax has been reported.
u: Starts as DRAFT; moved by tax staff and returns.
c: Only active transactions are included in a return.
v DRAFT: Calculated and not yet confirmed.
v ACTIVE: Confirmed and due to be reported.
v COMPLETED: Reported or settled. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn, for example after a credit. A final state.
r organization
s: The organisation the tax is reported by.
u: Chosen from the source document.
n: At most one organisation.
w: Decides which company's return includes it.

@ Team
s: A collaborative grouping of people used to assign work, roles, ownership and accountability.
b: Teams let work and responsibility be given to a group rather than a single person, and let access and reporting follow the group. A team belongs to an organisation, so each unit manages its own groups.
u: Set up by managers or administrators; chosen on tasks and records; read in reports.
c: A team belongs to one organisation.
l: A team is created, used while it works together, and left unused when disbanded. History keeps its name.
x: The "Warehouse Leeds" team is responsible for stock counts at the Leeds site.
a teamId
s: The identity of the team, constant when it is renamed.
b: Lets assignments and ownership point at the team rather than its name.
u: Generated on creation.
c: Referenced by tasks and records owned by the team.
a code
s: The short code of the team, such as WH-LDS.
b: Gives a stable way to refer to the team in imports and rules.
u: Unique; used in imports and rules.
c: Read with the name.
a name
s: The name of the team as people know it.
b: What people look for when assigning work.
u: Shown on assignments.
c: Read with the organisation.
r organization
s: The organisation the team belongs to.
u: Chosen when the team is created.
n: Exactly one organisation.
w: Decides whose structure the team is part of.
