@ Protocol
a status
v DRAFT: Being written; not yet approved for use.
v APPROVED: Cleared for use by studies and experiments.
v SUSPENDED: Withdrawn from use for a time, for example pending a review.
v RETIRED: Superseded or no longer used. A final state.

@ PurchaseOrderLine
a priceSource
v PRICE_LIST: Taken from the supplier's price list.
v CONTRACT: Taken from a contract with the supplier.
v SUPPLIER_AGREEMENT: Taken from a standing supplier agreement.
v QUOTATION: Taken from an accepted supplier quotation.
v MANUAL: Entered by the buyer.
v OTHER: Determined some other way.
