@ FuelTransaction
a quantity
s: The amount of fuel or energy supplied to the vehicle in this fill, stated in the transaction's unit of measure.
u: Taken from the pump receipt or fuel-card feed; must be positive, and consumption and efficiency reports divide distance by it.
a totalAmount
s: The total price paid for the fuel or energy supplied, in the transaction's currency.
u: Taken from the receipt or card statement and must not be negative; fleet cost allocation and payment reconciliation read it.
a occurredAt
s: The date and time at which the vehicle was actually fuelled.
u: Captured from the pump or card feed rather than the entry time; efficiency, route and fraud checks compare it with vehicle movements.
a odometerOrMeterReading
s: The vehicle's odometer or usage-meter reading taken at the time of fuelling.
u: Entered by the driver or read from telematics; consumption per distance and maintenance schedules depend on consecutive readings.

@ GateEvent
a occurredAt
s: The moment the container, vehicle or visit passed through the gate or was processed there.
u: Taken from the gate system's own timestamp; dwell time, security review and billing disputes are resolved against it.
a direction
s: Whether the movement entered the controlled area, left it, or passed between internal zones.
u: Set by the gate system at capture; yard inventory and custody transfer change according to it.
v IN: Entry into the controlled terminal area, which starts the subject's time inside.
v OUT: Exit from the controlled terminal area, which ends the subject's time inside.
v INTERNAL: A gate-controlled move between zones inside the terminal, with no entry or exit.
v OTHER: Another governed gate occurrence that is neither entry, exit nor internal move.

@ Gate
a name
s: The descriptive name of the gate, such as North Truck Gate, as staff and drivers know it.
u: Entered at set-up and shown on operations screens and reports; it can change while the gate code stays the key.

@ GoodsReceiptLine
a goodsReceiptLineId
s: The permanent identity of the receipt line, unchanged by corrections to its quantities or notes.
u: Generated on creation; inventory movements, invoice matching lines and supplier returns refer to the line by this value.
a lineNumber
s: The position of the line within its goods receipt, used to cite it in documents and queries.
u: Assigned in sequence when the line is added and unique within the receipt; printed on delivery paperwork and audit extracts.
a receivedQuantity
s: The quantity physically counted at the dock for this line, before any quality decision.
u: Entered by receiving staff; it should equal accepted, rejected and pending-inspection quantities together, and drives order fulfilment.
a acceptedQuantity
s: The part of the received quantity that passed receiving controls and may be taken into stock.
u: Set after inspection; it posts to inventory, counts toward purchase order fulfilment and is the basis for invoice matching.
a rejectedQuantity
s: The part of the received quantity refused because of damage, wrong item or failed quality checks.
u: Entered with the rejection reason by quality staff; it opens supplier disputes and returns and never enters inventory.
a pendingInspectionQuantity
s: The part of the received quantity held aside until inspection or disposition decides its fate.
u: Set at receipt and reduced as inspection moves quantities to accepted or rejected; zero when inspection is complete.
a notes
s: Free-text receiving remarks about this line, such as damage observed, carrier comments or partial shipment explanations.
u: Written by receiving staff at the dock; read by quality, purchasing and auditors when reviewing the delivery.
