@ HandlingUnit
a handlingUnitId
s: The permanent identity of the handling unit, kept as it moves between locations and shipments.
u: Generated on creation; inventory, picking, packing and shipment records refer to the unit by this value.
a handlingUnitNumber
s: The scannable number on the unit's label, such as a pallet licence plate, by which warehouse staff identify it.
u: Unique; printed on the label at creation and scanned during putaway, picking, staging and loading.
a type
s: The physical form of the unit, such as a pallet or carton, which fixes its size and handling equipment.
u: Chosen when the unit is created; capacity, equipment, packing and carrier planning read it.
v PALLET: A palletised load moved by forklift or pallet truck.
v CARTON: A cardboard box or case holding items for storage or shipment.
v TOTE: A reusable bin or tote used for picking and internal movement.
v CAGE: A roll cage or similar wheeled frame used to move mixed goods.
v DRUM: A cylindrical drum or barrel holding liquids or bulk material.
v PACKAGE: A general parcel that is none of the other forms.
v OTHER: A governed unit type not covered by the listed forms.

@ HealthcareEncounter
a encounterId
s: The permanent identity of the encounter, kept for the whole of the patient's visit or stay.
u: Generated on creation; diagnoses, procedures, orders and clinical notes refer to the encounter by this value.
a encounterType
v OUTPATIENT: A visit without an overnight stay.
v INPATIENT: Care with admission to a ward and an overnight stay.
v EMERGENCY: Unplanned urgent care given to a patient arriving with an acute problem.
v VIRTUAL: Care delivered remotely by video or telephone.
v HOME_CARE: Care delivered at the patient's home.
v OTHER: Care given in a setting that fits none of the listed ones.
a status
s: Where the encounter is in its course, from planned through care being delivered to completion.
u: Updated by the provider as care starts and ends; billing and coding act only on completed encounters.
a startAt
s: The date and time at which the encounter began and care started.
u: Recorded at check-in or when care begins; lengths of stay, scheduling analysis and billing periods run from it.

@ HealthcareOrder
a healthcareOrderId
s: The permanent identity of the order, kept unchanged through issue, performance and any amendment.
u: Generated on creation; results, billing items and the ordering encounter refer to it by this value.
a status
s: Where the order stands, from being prepared to carried out or withdrawn.
u: Moved by the ordering clinician and the performing team; completed orders are amended or replaced, never rewritten.

@ HealthcarePatient
a status
v ACTIVE: A current patient who receives or is eligible for care.
v INACTIVE: Not currently receiving care; the record is kept and can be reactivated.
v DECEASED: The patient has died; no new care is planned. A final state.
v MERGED: A duplicate record that has been merged into another; use the surviving record. A final state.
