@ Identity
a identityType
s: The authentication method this identity uses to prove who the user is when signing in or calling the application.
u: Chosen by a security administrator when the identity is created; it decides which checks run at sign-in and which other fields must be filled.
v PASSWORD: A secret password held and verified by the application itself.
v SSO: A single sign-on account vouched for by the organisation's identity provider.
v OAUTH: An account at an external OAuth provider that the application trusts to confirm the person.
v API_KEY: A key presented by software to call the application on behalf of a service or integration.
v CERTIFICATE: A client certificate whose private key proves identity, typically for machines or high-assurance staff access.
v BIOMETRIC: A fingerprint, face scan or similar physical trait checked by a device or service.
v OTHER: Any authentication mechanism not covered by the other types, described in the identity's notes.
@ InspectionSample
a unitOfMeasure
s: The unit in which the sample quantity is expressed, such as grams, millilitres or pieces.
u: Chosen by the inspector when the sample is taken; quantities are reconciled, converted and reported against it, and it stays fixed for audit.
@ InsuranceClaim
a claimNumber
s: The reference number that identifies the claim for both the claimant and the insurer in all correspondence.
u: Allocated by the system when the claim is registered; unique, never reused, and quoted on every call, letter and payment.
a claimedAmount
s: The monetary amount the claimant asks the insurer to pay for the loss, as stated on the claim.
u: Entered by the claims handler from the claim form; must not be negative, and the assessed or approved amount is compared against it.
a status
s: Where the claim stands in the insurer's handling, from first report through assessment to payment or refusal.
u: Moved by claims handlers as the claim is assessed and paid; later states depend on the outcome of review, and denied or closed claims are not reopened.
v REPORTED: The claim has been received and registered but assessment has not yet started.
v UNDER_REVIEW: A handler is assessing cover, evidence and the amount that is owed.
v APPROVED: Accepted for the full claimed amount and awaiting payment.
v PARTIALLY_APPROVED: Accepted for less than the claimed amount, with the difference not payable.
v DENIED: Refused, for example because the loss is not covered. A final state.
v SETTLED: Payment has been made to the claimant.
v CLOSED: Finished with nothing further to do. A final state.
@ InsurancePolicy
a policyNumber
s: The unique number printed on the policy documents and used to identify the contract.
u: Allocated by the insurer when the policy is quoted or issued; unique, and quoted when claims are reported and premiums collected.
a status
u: Moved by underwriting and policy administration; claims are accepted only under ACTIVE policies, and EXPIRED or CANCELLED policies cannot be reinstated.
v QUOTED: Offered to the customer but not yet accepted, so no cover is in place.
v ACTIVE: In force, so claims for covered events within the period are accepted.
v SUSPENDED: Cover is paused, for example because a premium is unpaid, and can be restored.
v EXPIRED: The policy term has ended without renewal. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Ended early, or withdrawn while still a quote. A final state.
