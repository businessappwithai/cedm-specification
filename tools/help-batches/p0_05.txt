@ ConstructionProject
a status
v PLANNED: Approved but design has not begun.
v DESIGN: Design and permitting are under way.
v PROCUREMENT: Contractors and materials are being sourced.
v CONSTRUCTION: Physical work is being carried out on site.
v COMMISSIONING: Building work is done and systems are being tested and handed over.
v COMPLETED: Handed over to the owner and closed. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Stopped before completion, with work abandoned or the contract ended. A final state.
@ ContractObligation
a obligationType
v DELIVERY: Goods or deliverables must be delivered.
v PAYMENT: Money must be paid.
v SERVICE: A service must be performed.
v REPORTING: Information or reports must be supplied.
v COMPLIANCE: A legal, regulatory or standards requirement must be met.
v PERFORMANCE: A level of performance, such as a service level, must be achieved.
v OTHER: Any other commitment that does not fit the listed kinds.
a status
v OPEN: Due but not yet started.
v IN_PROGRESS: Being worked on by the responsible party, with fulfilment not yet shown.
v FULFILLED: Met; evidence is on record. A final state.
v BREACHED: Not met in time or at the required standard. A final state that may lead to remedies.
v WAIVED: Released by the other party; no longer required. A final state.
v CANCELLED: No longer applicable, for example because the contract changed. A final state.
@ Control
a name
s: The short name of the control as it appears in the control register, such as Monthly bank reconciliation review.
u: Written by the risk and compliance team when the control is defined; shown in registers, test plans and audit reports.
a frequency
v CONTINUOUS: Operates all the time, for example an automated system check.
v DAILY: Performed every day.
v WEEKLY: Performed every week.
v MONTHLY: Performed every month.
v QUARTERLY: Performed every quarter of the year.
v ANNUAL: Performed once a year.
v EVENT_DRIVEN: Performed when a particular event occurs.
v AD_HOC: Performed as needed, with no fixed schedule.
@ CorrectiveActionVerification
a verificationNumber
s: The unique reference number that quality staff and suppliers quote for this effectiveness check.
u: Assigned when the verification is raised; used in approvals, audit trails and supplier correspondence.
a result
s: The verifier's conclusion on whether the action achieved its intended result.
u: Recorded when verification completes; an effective result can support closing the action, while other results force further work or an exception.
a status
s: Where the verification is, from being raised through the check to completion, reopening or cancellation.
u: Moved by the independent verifier; a verification can only complete once a result and findings are recorded.
v OPEN: Raised but not started.
v IN_PROGRESS: The verification is being carried out.
v COMPLETED: Finished with a result and findings recorded. A final state.
v REOPENED: Reopened because new evidence or a recurrence needs further verification.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn and not carried out. A final state.
@ CorrectiveAction
a correctiveActionId
s: The permanent system identity of one corrective or preventive action.
u: Generated on creation; verifications, nonconformances and audits refer to the action through it.
a actionNumber
s: The unique reference number by which people refer to the action in CAPA meetings and reports.
u: Assigned when the action is raised; quoted in supplier communication, tracking dashboards and integrations.
a actionType
s: The purpose of the action: containing a problem, fixing the item, removing its cause, or preventing recurrence elsewhere.
u: Chosen by the quality owner; decides what evidence is expected, who reviews it and how effectiveness is judged.
a status
v OPEN: Raised but not yet assigned.
v ASSIGNED: Given to an owner, who has not started.
v IN_PROGRESS: Being carried out by the owner, with implementation not yet finished.
v VERIFICATION: Done and waiting for an effectiveness check.
v COMPLETED: Done and, where required, verified effective. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn and not carried out. A final state.
a dueDate
s: The date by which the action should be implemented.
u: Agreed when the action is assigned; drives reminders and escalation, and overdue counts in quality governance reports.
a completedDate
s: The date implementation was formally finished, which may differ from the verification date.
u: Recorded when the action completes and required then; CAPA metrics measure time to complete against the due date.
