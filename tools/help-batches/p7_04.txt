@ SubscriptionPlan
a status
s: Whether the plan is still being defined, on sale, retired after its run, or withdrawn.
u: Starts as DRAFT; pricing managers activate it to offer it, and only an active plan can be chosen for new subscriptions.
v DRAFT: The plan is being defined and cannot yet be sold.
v ACTIVE: The plan is on offer and customers can subscribe to it.
v COMPLETED: The plan has run its course and is no longer offered, though existing subscriptions keep their terms. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The plan was withdrawn without ever being fully offered. A final state.

@ Subscription
a status
s: Where the customer's subscription is in its life: being set up, running, finished or cancelled.
u: Starts as DRAFT; account management activates it at sign-up, and billing and entitlements apply only while it is active.
v DRAFT: The subscription is being set up and does not yet bill or grant access.
v ACTIVE: The subscription is running; charges are billed and entitlements are granted.
v COMPLETED: The subscription reached its end date or fulfilled its term. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The subscription was ended early by the customer or the business. A final state.
