@ Routing
a status
v DRAFT: Being defined by engineering; not yet usable for production.
v ACTIVE: Approved for production and eligible for new orders to be released against.
v SUSPENDED: Temporarily withdrawn from use, for example pending a process investigation.
v OBSOLETE: Superseded by a newer version or no longer used. A final state.
@ ServiceRequest
a status
v OPEN: Received and not yet looked at.
v TRIAGED: Assessed and given a priority.
v ASSIGNED: Given to a named person or team to handle.
v IN_PROGRESS: Being actively worked on by the assignee.
v RESOLVED: The need has been met, awaiting confirmation.
v CLOSED: Confirmed finished. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was resolved. A final state.
