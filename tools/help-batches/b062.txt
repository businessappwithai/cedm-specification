@ Settlement
a settlementNumber
s: The number of the settlement, such as STL-3041.
a amount
c: Compared with the approved claim amount and the payment made.
a status
s: Where the settlement is in approval and execution.
v DRAFT: Being prepared; nothing is committed.
v APPROVED: Approved and ready to be carried out.
v EXECUTED: Paid or otherwise discharged. A final state.
v VOID: Cancelled and of no effect. A final state.
r claim
n: A settlement discharges one insurance claim; separate claims are settled separately.
r claimLines
n: At least one claim line is settled; each line is covered by the settlement.
r currency
n: The amount is in exactly one currency.

@ Shipment
a status
v PLANNED: Intended; no transport is booked.
v BOOKED: A carrier is booked and a date agreed.
v IN_TRANSIT: On its way.
v DELIVERED: Arrived at the destination. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Called off before delivery. A final state.
v EXCEPTION: Delayed or damaged; needs a decision.
a shipmentId
s: The identity of the shipment, constant for its life.
u: Generated on creation; lines and tracking refer to it.
c: Referenced by every line and movement of the shipment.
a shipmentType
s: The direction of the movement.
u: Chosen when the shipment is created; decides which orders can link.
c: Read with the origin and destination.
v INBOUND: Goods arriving from a supplier.
v OUTBOUND: Goods going to a customer.
v TRANSFER: Goods moving between the business's own locations.
v RETURN: Goods coming back from a customer or going back to a supplier.
a shipmentNumber
c: Read with the carrier's reference to follow a delivery.
u: Unique; quoted to carriers and customers.
a plannedDate
s: The date the shipment is planned to be delivered.
u: Set when booked; used to plan receiving and dispatch.
c: Compared with the actual date.
a actualDate
s: The date the shipment was actually delivered.
u: Set on delivery.
c: Gives the delivery performance.
a trackingReference
s: The carrier's tracking number.
u: Entered when booked; given to the customer.
c: Used to follow the shipment.
r origin
s: The place the goods leave from.
u: Chosen when planned.
n: At most one location.
t: Starts the journey.
r destination
s: The place the goods are going to.
u: Chosen when planned.
n: At most one location.
t: Ends the journey.
r carrier
s: The party transporting the goods.
u: Chosen when booked.
n: At most one carrier.
t: Who is paid and tracked.
r containers
s: The containers carrying the goods.
u: Chosen when goods are containerised.
n: Any number of containers.
t: Identify the units that are moved.
r salesOrder
s: The sales order the shipment delivers.
u: Chosen for outbound shipments.
n: At most one order.
t: Links delivery to the sale.
r purchaseOrder
s: The purchase order the shipment delivers against.
u: Chosen for inbound shipments.
n: At most one order.
t: Links receipt to the purchase.
r inventoryMovements
s: The stock movements caused by the shipment.
u: Read to reconcile stock.
n: Any number of movements.
t: Record the stock effect.

@ ShipmentLine
r shipment
n: Each line belongs to exactly one shipment and goes with it.
r product
n: Each line carries one product.
a lineNumber
s: The position of the line on the shipment.
r inventoryMovements
u: Read to reconcile the line with the stock movements that moved the goods.
