@ Fee
a status
s: Where the education fee stands, from preparation and assessment through invoicing to settlement, waiver or cancellation.
u: Moved by student accounts and billing staff; reports read it to separate open charges from settled ones.
@ Guardian
a relationshipType
v PARENT: A biological or adoptive parent.
v LEGAL_GUARDIAN: A person appointed with legal responsibility for the student.
v CAREGIVER: A person who cares for the student day to day without legal guardianship.
v SPONSOR: A person or body that supports the student financially.
v OTHER: Any relationship to the student not covered by the other types.
