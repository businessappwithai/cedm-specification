@ CustomerReturnLine
a quantityRestocked
s: How many returned units passed inspection and may go back into unrestricted, saleable stock.
u: Entered at disposition by the warehouse; it alone drives the positive stock movement, and it is part of the quantity returned.
a quantityQuarantined
s: How many returned units are held in restricted stock until further inspection or controlled handling.
u: Entered at disposition by quality staff; it never adds to available stock, and it is part of the quantity returned.
a quantityToRepair
s: How many returned units are sent for repair or refurbishment before any decision on their stock.
u: Entered at disposition; it may raise a repair work order and adds nothing to unrestricted stock, and it is part of the quantity returned.
a quantityScrapped
s: How many returned units are approved for scrap or disposal and will never return to saleable stock.
u: Entered at disposition by the warehouse; it feeds disposal and inventory write-off, and it is part of the quantity returned.
@ CustomerReturn
a status
s: Where the return stands, from request and authorisation through transport, receipt, inspection and completion.
u: Moved by customer service and the warehouse; it controls what may happen next but does not itself change stock or invoices.
a notes
s: Free-text remarks about the return, such as damage seen on arrival or what the customer said.
u: Written by customer service or warehouse staff for colleagues and audit; the facts that matter are held in structured fields.
@ Customer
a partyRoleId
s: The link to the party role that makes this party a customer.
u: Set when the customer is created; the party's name, addresses and contacts are read through it and are not copied here.
a customerCode
s: The short code staff and systems use to refer to this customer.
u: Assigned when the customer is set up; quoted on orders, invoices and statements and used in integrations, and different from the system id.
a customerType
s: The commercial kind of buyer, such as a private consumer, a company or a public body.
u: Chosen by sales when the customer is created; it steers pricing, credit rules, tax handling and reporting segments.
v INDIVIDUAL: A private consumer buying for personal use rather than for a business.
v BUSINESS: A company or other commercial organisation buying for its own operations.
v GOVERNMENT: A public authority or agency, often with its own procurement and payment rules.
v INTERNAL: Another unit of the organisation itself, supplied through internal sales.
v OTHER: A customer that fits none of the other kinds.
a creditStatus
s: The credit-control decision currently in force for this customer.
u: Set by credit control after review; order authorisation, receivables and collections check it before releasing credit-bearing orders.
a status
s: Whether the customer relationship is open for business, dormant, held back or closed.
u: Moved by sales or finance; it decides whether new orders may be taken, while past transactions stay attributed to the customer.
