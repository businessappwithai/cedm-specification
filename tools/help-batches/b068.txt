@ Supplier
r partyRole
n: A supplier is the supplier role of exactly one party role, which links it to the party's identity.

@ SupplierClaim
a supplierClaimId
s: The identity of the claim, constant for its life.
a claimNumber
s: The number of the claim, such as SCL-0231.
a status
s: Where the claim is in review and settlement.
v DRAFT: Being prepared; not yet sent to the supplier.
v OPEN: Raised with the supplier and awaiting review.
v UNDER_REVIEW: Being assessed by the supplier or the buyer.
v ACCEPTED: The supplier accepts the claim in full.
v PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED: The supplier accepts part of the claim.
v REJECTED: The supplier refuses the claim. A final state.
v RESOLVED: The agreed remedy has been carried out.
v CLOSED: Finished and archived. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn by the buyer. A final state.
v ESCALATED: Passed to a higher level because it is not agreed.
a claimType
s: What the claim is about.
v QUALITY: Goods do not meet the quality required.
v DAMAGE: Goods arrived damaged.
v SHORTAGE: Less was delivered than ordered.
v OVERAGE: More was delivered than ordered.
v WRONG_ITEM: A different item was delivered.
v WARRANTY: A fault covered by warranty.
v SERVICE: A service was not performed as agreed.
v COMMERCIAL: A dispute about price or terms.
v DELIVERY: Late or failed delivery.
v DOCUMENTATION: Missing or wrong paperwork.
v OTHER: A claim that fits no other type.
a resolutionCode
s: The outcome agreed for the claim.
v NO_ACTION: Nothing further is done.
v REPLACEMENT: The supplier sends replacement goods.
v REPAIR: The goods are repaired.
v RETURN: The goods are returned to the supplier.
v CREDIT: The supplier issues a credit.
v DEBIT_ADJUSTMENT: The buyer raises a debit adjustment.
v PRICE_ADJUSTMENT: The price is reduced.
v ACCEPTED_EXCEPTION: The buyer accepts the goods as they are.
v REJECTED: The claim was refused.

@ SupplierClaimResolution
a resolutionType
s: The remedy agreed.
v NO_ACTION: Nothing further is done.
v REPLACEMENT: The supplier sends replacement goods.
v REPAIR: The goods are repaired.
v RETURN: The goods are returned to the supplier.
v CREDIT: The supplier issues a credit.
v DEBIT_ADJUSTMENT: The buyer raises a debit adjustment.
v PRICE_ADJUSTMENT: The price is reduced.
v CASH_RECOVERY: The supplier pays back in cash.
v ACCEPTED_EXCEPTION: The buyer accepts the goods as they are.
a status
s: Where the resolution is in being carried out.
v DRAFT: Proposed and not yet approved.
v APPROVED: Agreed and ready to carry out.
v IN_EXECUTION: Being carried out.
v PARTIALLY_EXECUTED: Some of the remedy is done.
v EXECUTED: The remedy is fully done. A final state.
v FAILED: Could not be carried out. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn. A final state.

@ SupplierCreditNote
a supplierCreditNoteId
s: The identity of the credit note, constant for its life.

@ SupplierCreditNoteApplication
a status
s: Whether the application is in force.
v DRAFT: Prepared and not yet applied.
v ACTIVE: Applied against the invoice, reducing what is owed.
v REVERSED: Undone by a reversing application. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before it was applied. A final state.
