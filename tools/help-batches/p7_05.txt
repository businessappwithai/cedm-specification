@ SupplierClaimResolution
a resolutionNumber
s: The business reference of the resolution decision, used with the supplier and internal teams.
u: Assigned when the resolution is drafted; unique; quoted in approvals, correspondence and reconciliation, and distinct from the claim number.
a resolutionDate
s: The date and time the agreed remedy was authorized and became effective.
u: Set at approval; used for SLA reporting and financial control, and it anchors the start of downstream execution.
a resolutionType
s: The remedy agreed with the supplier for the claim, such as replacement, credit or cash recovery.
u: Chosen when the resolution is drafted; it routes the downstream work and decides which execution evidence is required.
v NO_ACTION: The claim was reviewed and no remedy is owed or taken.
v REPLACEMENT: The supplier will deliver replacement goods or service for the faulty ones.
v REPAIR: The supplier will repair the faulty goods and return them.
v RETURN: The goods are returned to the supplier, which normally needs a SupplierReturn.
v CREDIT: The supplier issues a credit note that reduces what is owed to it.
v DEBIT_ADJUSTMENT: The buyer raises a debit note against the supplier to recover the amount.
v PRICE_ADJUSTMENT: The price of the supplied goods is reduced and the goods are kept.
v CASH_RECOVERY: The supplier pays the agreed amount back as cash, evidenced by a Payment.
v ACCEPTED_EXCEPTION: The buyer accepts the nonconformity as an approved exception without recovery.
a status
s: How far the agreed remedy has been carried out, from draft and approval to execution or failure.
u: Starts as DRAFT; approval releases the downstream work, and the status shows whether the required consequences completed.
v DRAFT: The resolution is being prepared and has not been authorized.
v APPROVED: The remedy is authorized but execution has not started.
v IN_EXECUTION: Downstream work such as returns, credits or payments is under way.
v PARTIALLY_EXECUTED: Part of the remedy has been carried out and the rest is outstanding.
v EXECUTED: Every required consequence of the remedy is complete. A final state.
v FAILED: Execution could not be completed, for example because the supplier did not comply. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The resolution was withdrawn before completion. A final state.
a approvedAmount
s: The money the organization is authorized to recover from the supplier under this resolution.
u: Set at approval from the negotiated outcome; it is the target that credit notes, debit notes or payments are reconciled against.

@ SupplierClaim
a claimType
s: The main nature of the claim, such as quality, damage, shortage or a commercial dispute.
u: Chosen when the claim is opened; it drives routing, required evidence, supplier scorecards and analytics.
v QUALITY: Goods or services do not meet quality or specification requirements.
v DAMAGE: Goods arrived damaged or were damaged in transit or handling.
v SHORTAGE: Less was delivered than ordered or invoiced.
v OVERAGE: More was delivered than ordered, creating excess stock.
v WRONG_ITEM: A different product from the one ordered was delivered.
v WARRANTY: A defect is claimed under the supplier's warranty after acceptance.
v SERVICE: A purchased service was not performed as agreed.
v COMMERCIAL: The dispute concerns price, terms or charges rather than the goods.
v DELIVERY: Goods arrived late, early or to the wrong place.
v DOCUMENTATION: Required certificates, invoices or paperwork are missing or wrong.
v OTHER: A claim that fits none of the other types, explained in the notes.
a notes
s: Free-text narrative of the claim, covering what happened and what was discussed with the supplier.
u: Written by the claim handler and updated during investigation; supports escalation and audit but should not replace structured fields.
