@ Vessel
a status
v ACTIVE: In service and available for voyages.
v INACTIVE: Not currently in use; can return to service.
v OUT_OF_SERVICE: Unusable, for example under repair.
v RETIRED: Withdrawn from the fleet. A final state.
r voyages
n: Any number of voyages are made by a vessel over its life.
