@ RegulatorySubmission
s: A formal submission to a regulatory authority to obtain an approval, authorisation or registration, or to meet a regulatory obligation.
b: A submission is the business's record of what it filed with a regulator, when, and what came of it. Keeping the stages and the documents together proves what was submitted, shows where it stands, and keeps the questions and decision on file.
u: Prepared by regulatory affairs; submitted to the authority; updated as the authority responds.
c: A submission is made to one authority, may be on behalf of a sponsor, and includes documents.
l: A submission is drafted and submitted, goes under review, and may be sent back with questions that are answered. It ends approved, rejected or withdrawn, all final.
x: Submission RS-2026-031 asks the authority to register a new formulation; it is under review until questions arrive.
a regulatorySubmissionId
s: The identity of the submission, constant for its life.
u: Generated on creation; documents and correspondence refer to it.
c: Referenced by the documents filed with it.
a submissionNumber
s: The number the business gives the submission, such as RS-2026-031.
u: Unique; quoted in correspondence with the authority.
c: Read with the authority's own reference number if any.
a submissionType
s: The kind of submission, such as registration or renewal.
u: Entered when the submission is created; chooses the documents required.
c: Decides which authority procedures apply.
a status
s: Where the submission is in the authority's process.
u: Starts as DRAFT; moved as the submission is filed and answered.
c: Decides whether documents can still be added.
v DRAFT: Being prepared; not yet filed.
v SUBMITTED: Filed with the authority.
v UNDER_REVIEW: The authority is reviewing it.
v QUESTIONS: The authority has asked for more information.
v APPROVED: The authority approved it. A final state.
v REJECTED: The authority refused it. A final state.
v WITHDRAWN: Withdrawn by the submitter before a decision. A final state.
a submittedAt
s: When the submission was filed with the authority.
u: Set on filing; required once the submission is no longer a draft.
c: Starts the review period.
a decisionAt
s: When the authority decided.
u: Set when the submission is approved or rejected.
c: Measured against the filing date to give review time.
r authority
s: The regulator the submission is made to.
u: Chosen when the submission is created.
n: Exactly one authority.
t: Decides the rules and the format required.
r sponsor
s: The organisation on whose behalf the submission is made.
u: Chosen when it differs from the submitting company.
n: At most one sponsor.
t: Names who holds the resulting approval.
r documents
s: The documents filed in support of the submission.
u: Attached before filing and with later answers.
n: Any number of documents.
t: Forms the evidence the authority reviews.

@ Renewal
s: A subscription or service continuation that extends a customer's entitlement into the next term.
b: A renewal is the decision point at the end of a term. Recording it lets the business offer, confirm and track continuation, and decide whether the customer keeps the service, without altering the billing or entitlement records that carry out the result.
u: Created by account management ahead of the term end; read by sales, finance and support.
c: A renewal concerns one customer and the subscription or service being continued.
l: A renewal is drafted, becomes active while it is being worked, and completes when the new term is in place. It is cancelled if the customer does not continue.
x: In October the "Platform Premium" renewal for a customer is drafted, confirmed in November and completed when the new term starts.
a renewalId
s: The identity of the renewal, constant for its life.
u: Generated on creation; billing and entitlement records refer to it.
a status
s: Where the renewal is.
u: Starts as DRAFT; moved by account management.
v DRAFT: Being prepared; nothing is committed yet.
v ACTIVE: Offered or confirmed and being carried out.
v COMPLETED: The new term is in place. A final state.
v CANCELLED: The customer did not continue. A final state.
r customer
s: The customer whose subscription is being renewed.
u: Chosen when the renewal is created.
n: At most one customer; empty until identified.
w: Decides whom to invoice and contact.
