@ BusinessProcess
r ownerOrganization
u: Named by process management when the process is defined; the owner approves changes to it and answers for how well it performs.
r workflows
u: Linked when a workflow is built to carry out the process, so analysts can see which automations implement each step.
r tasks
u: Tasks are attached as the process runs; managers read them to see progress, workload and where work is waiting.
r documents
u: Attached by the process owner so staff can open the current procedure, form or policy while they carry out the process.
@ BusinessUnit
a name
s: The full name of the business unit, as it appears in the organisation's structure and management accounts.
u: Entered by management or finance when the unit is created; shown in organisation charts, selectors and segment reports.
@ Calendar
a name
s: The descriptive name of the calendar, such as UK Working Days or Group Fiscal Calendar.
u: Entered by the administrator who maintains it; shown wherever a schedule, service level or plan asks which calendar applies.
@ CashPosition
a cashPositionId
s: The permanent system identity of one cash snapshot for a bank account at a cut-off time.
u: Generated when the position is calculated; forecasts, funding decisions and audit trails refer to that snapshot through it.
a asOf
s: The date and time of the cut-off for which balances and pending items were calculated.
u: Chosen when the snapshot is taken, such as end of day or an intraday check; the position must be reproducible from evidence at that moment.
@ CertificateOfAnalysis
a certificateOfAnalysisId
s: The permanent system identity of one certificate of analysis.
u: Generated on creation; shipments, receipts and customer exchanges refer to the certificate through it, whatever its printed number.
a certificateNumber
s: The unique number printed on the certificate, which customers and auditors quote when asking for it.
u: Assigned by quality when the certificate is drafted; used for search, document exchange and audit, and never reused.
a issuedAt
s: The date and time the certificate was released to its recipient, such as a customer or receiving site.
u: Recorded when the status becomes issued and required by then; customers and auditors rely on it as the release date.
a status
s: Where the certificate stands in controlled release, from drafting to issue and eventual replacement or withdrawal.
u: Moved by quality staff; only issued certificates are sent to customers, and corrections create a replacement rather than an edit.
v DRAFT: Being prepared from the inspection results; not yet approved.
v APPROVED: Approved by quality but not yet released to the customer.
v ISSUED: Released to the recipient; it now stands as evidence.
v SUPERSEDED: Replaced by a corrected certificate; kept for history. A final state.
v VOID: Withdrawn because it was wrong or issued in error; kept for history. A final state.
@ ChemicalBatch
a status
v PLANNED: Scheduled to be produced or received; no material exists yet.
v QUARANTINED: Present but held until quality checks pass; not usable.
v RELEASED: Passed quality checks and available for use.
v REJECTED: Failed quality checks; must not be used. A final state.
v EXPIRED: Past its expiry date and no longer usable. A final state.
v CONSUMED: Fully used up, with no material left to issue. A final state.
