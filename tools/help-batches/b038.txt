@ Premium
a premiumId
s: The identity of the premium record.
u: Generated when the premium is assessed; invoices and payments refer to it.
a premiumNumber
s: The number of the premium notice, quoted to the policyholder.
u: Allocated from a number series; unique.
a amount
s: The premium amount due for the period.
u: Calculated from the policy's rating; not negative.
c: Expressed in the premium's currency; the invoice, if one is raised, repeats it.
a dueDate
s: The date by which the premium must be paid.
u: Set from the billing schedule; reminders and lapse rules run from it.
a status
s: Where the premium stands.
u: Moved by policy billing and finance.
v DRAFT: Calculated but not yet due.
v DUE: Payable now.
v INVOICED: Included on an invoice to the policyholder.
v PAID: Received. A final state.
v WAIVED: Excused by the insurer. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn, for example because the policy was cancelled. A final state.
r policy
n: Exactly one policy: a premium is the price of the cover under a specific policy.
r insuredParty
s: The insured party the premium is charged for, where cover is priced per person.
u: Set for group and family policies.
n: At most one insured party.
w: Lets a premium be traced to the person it covers.
r currency
n: Exactly one currency, so the amount is unambiguous.

@ Prescription
s: An authorised instruction for a patient to receive or use a medication or therapy.
b: The prescription turns a clinical decision into an instruction that pharmacists and nurses can follow safely: who gets what, how much, how often and for how long.
u: Written by a prescriber after assessment; dispensed and administered; read by pharmacy, nursing and the patient.
c: A prescription is for a HealthcarePatient, names a Medication and is issued by a HealthcareProvider.
l: A prescription is drafted, becomes active when signed, and ends completed when the course is finished, discontinued by the prescriber, or cancelled if never used.
x: Amoxicillin 500 mg, three times daily by mouth for seven days, prescribed after a chest infection diagnosis.
a prescriptionId
s: The identity of the prescription.
u: Generated on creation; dispensing records refer to it.
a prescriptionNumber
s: The number printed on the prescription.
u: Allocated on signing; unique; presented at the pharmacy.
a dosage
s: How much medication is given each time, such as 500 mg.
u: Entered by the prescriber; checked against safe ranges.
a frequency
s: How often the dose is given, such as three times daily.
u: Entered by the prescriber; drives the administration schedule.
a route
s: How the medication is given, such as oral, intravenous or topical.
u: Chosen by the prescriber; must suit the medication's form.
a startDate
s: The first day of treatment.
u: Set by the prescriber; defaults to today.
a endDate
s: The last day of treatment.
u: Set by the prescriber; must not be before the start date.
a status
s: Where the prescription stands.
u: Moved by the prescriber and pharmacy.
v DRAFT: Being written; not yet valid.
v ACTIVE: Signed and in force.
v COMPLETED: The course is finished. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Withdrawn before use. A final state.
v DISCONTINUED: Stopped by the prescriber during treatment. A final state.
r patient
s: The patient who is to receive the medication.
u: Set at creation.
n: Exactly one patient: a prescription is for a specific person.
w: Adds the prescription to the patient's record for interaction and allergy checks.
r medication
s: The medication prescribed.
u: Chosen from the formulary.
n: Exactly one medication.
w: Defines the product to be dispensed.
r prescriber
s: The provider who issued the prescription.
u: Set when signed.
n: Exactly one prescriber, who is responsible for the instruction.
w: Identifies who to contact about it.

@ Pricing
s: A pricing definition or decision context that establishes base prices and the conditions under which they apply, before discounts, promotions, quotes or orders.
b: Price is not a single number: it depends on product, customer, quantity and date. A pricing definition holds those conditions so orders and quotes can find the right base price and explain it.
u: Maintained by pricing managers; looked up when quotes and orders are priced.
c: Quotes, orders and discount rules start from the base price that this definition gives.
l: Changes and retries preserve historical evidence and do not duplicate commercial effects.
x: The 2026 list price for the product family in the European market.
a pricingId
s: The identity of the pricing definition.
u: Generated on creation; price lists and quotes refer to it.
a code
s: The reference of the pricing definition, such as LIST-EU-2026.
u: Assigned by pricing managers; used to select the pricing.

@ Procedure
s: A clinical procedure that is planned or performed as part of healthcare delivery.
b: Procedures are the interventions that change a patient's condition, from a blood draw to an operation. Recording them, with codes and times, supports care continuity, billing and quality review.
u: Planned during an encounter; recorded when performed; read by clinicians, coders and billing.
c: A procedure is part of a HealthcareEncounter, is for a HealthcarePatient and is carried out by a HealthcareProvider.
l: A procedure is planned, in progress while performed, and completed; it can be cancelled before it happens.
x: Appendectomy, coded in the procedure coding system and performed on 12 May during an inpatient stay.
a procedureId
s: The identity of the procedure record.
u: Generated on creation.
a codeSystem
s: The coding system that the procedure code is from, such as CPT or SNOMED CT.
u: Chosen with the code; both are needed to interpret it.
a code
s: The code that identifies the procedure.
u: Selected by the clinician; used for billing and statistics.
a description
s: A plain-language description of the procedure.
u: Entered or filled from the coding system.
a status
s: Where the procedure stands.
u: Updated by the care team.
v PLANNED: Scheduled but not yet started.
v IN_PROGRESS: Being performed.
v COMPLETED: Done. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Not carried out. A final state.
a performedAt
s: When the procedure was performed.
u: Recorded when it takes place.
r encounter
s: The encounter during which the procedure took place.
u: Set at creation.
n: Exactly one encounter: a procedure happens during one episode of care.
w: Places the procedure in the clinical timeline.
r patient
s: The patient on whom the procedure is performed.
u: Set at creation.
n: Exactly one patient.
w: Adds it to the patient's record.
r performer
s: The provider who performed or will perform the procedure.
u: Set when assigned.
n: At most one performer recorded here; the team may be recorded elsewhere.
w: Identifies who is responsible for the procedure.
