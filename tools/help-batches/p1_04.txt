@ DiscountRule
a value
s: The size of the discount, read as a percentage or a money amount depending on the rule's method.
u: Set by pricing staff; for a percentage method it is the percent taken off, for a fixed-amount method it is money in the rule's currency.
@ DocumentClassification
a documentClassificationId
s: The stable identity of one classification, such as Confidential or Contract Record.
u: Generated when the classification is created; documents point to it so retention and access controls follow the label.
@ Document
a title
s: The heading of the document as readers know it, such as the subject of a letter or report.
u: Entered by the author or the originating process; shown in lists and search results, so it should say what the document is.
a updatedAt
s: The date and time the document record was last changed.
u: Set by the system on every change, never typed in; reviewers use it to tell whether they hold the latest version.
@ Driver
a driverCode
s: The short code that identifies this driver in dispatch and fleet systems.
u: Assigned when the driver is set up; used in scheduling, fuel records and reports, and unrelated to licence or person identifiers.
a status
s: Whether the driver is being onboarded, cleared to drive, barred for now, dormant, or gone.
u: Moved by fleet and compliance staff; only an ACTIVE driver is offered for assignment, and past trips keep their driver.
