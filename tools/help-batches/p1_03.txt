@ DataQualityAssessment
a status
s: How far the assessment has progressed from planning through checking to a recorded result.
u: Moved by the assessor as work proceeds; only a completed assessment is relied on when judging whether the dataset can be used.
v DRAFT: Being planned; the criteria and scope are not yet fixed.
v ACTIVE: Checks are running and results are being collected.
v COMPLETED: Finished with results recorded for the dataset. A final state.
v CANCELLED: Abandoned before completion, so no result stands. A final state.
@ Dataset
a name
s: The descriptive name by which the dataset is known in the data catalogue.
u: Chosen by the data owner when the dataset is registered; analysts search and recognise datasets by it, so it should be specific.
a status
s: Whether the dataset is being assembled, published for use, kept only for reference, or withdrawn.
u: Set by the data owner; only an active dataset is offered to users for new work, and archived or retired ones stay for history.
@ Department
a name
s: The name of the department as the organisation calls it, such as Finance or Field Operations.
u: Entered by an administrator; shown in organisation charts, on documents and in reports that group people and costs by department.
r organization
u: Chosen when the department is created; reporting lines, headcount and budgets roll up through the organisation it belongs to.
@ Diagnosis
r encounter
u: Set when the clinician records the finding; it shows when and in which visit the condition was identified, and who was involved.
r patient
u: Set from the encounter when the diagnosis is recorded; it adds the condition to the patient's history and drives treatment and coding.
