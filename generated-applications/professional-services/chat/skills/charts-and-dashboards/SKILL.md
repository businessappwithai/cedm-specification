# Charts and dashboards

A report may have a **chart** drawn from the same saved query. When it does,
the report card offers **Chart and report page**, which opens the reporting
platform's own page for that report — chart included — inside the
conversation.

Dashboards live in the reporting platform. The person can open
`/report` in a tab; they are already signed in there.

Do not describe a chart's shape from the first rows of a table: the preview is
a page of rows, not the whole series.
